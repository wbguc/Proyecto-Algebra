THE TRAVELER (return to top)

Currently traveling through Teyvat to find the truth of the world, and to reunite with their twin
Described as the ‘Child of Dandelions’ by the Aranara
Liloupar knows that they’re an exiled Prince/Princess
Ei found out that they’re a star after being told
The two Watchers of Temple of Space seem to know the Traveler
Melanta says the Traveler is not the child of humanity
Nihilita says they’re a “returnee from the sea of stars”
The last descendant of a subjugated civilization that forced the twins to travel in their spaceship to find a new world with “potential” to rebuild their civilization
The ship’s maintenance, course, and choice of destination was left under the control of an entity speculated to be an extremely sophisticated A.I.
It traveled for thousands of years while the twins hibernated within the Hibernation Chamber
The ship is a pioneer vessel, capable of terraforming an entire planet; modifying its lithosphere, biosphere, and atmosphere, and equipped with a genetic vault
It flew past Teyvat thousands of years ago, but didn’t land
As long as their attitude of kindness and nobleness remain, they will always have companions
Crash-landed in Nod-Krai eons ago as the Fourth Descender, and remained asleep within the Hibernation Chamber for an unknown amount of time
Awakened by their twin 500 years ago despite the doors never being opened
Their powers sealed after battling against the Shade of Space; Asmoday, when they wanted to leave Teyvat, and was put to sleep again for another 500 years
Befriended a fairy named Paimon 2 months before the start of the game
Paimon was fished out by the Traveler after she got exhausted one day and was caught in a strange whirlpool before being sucked out to sea
Not much of a talker and prefers to keep things bottled up
A lot older than they look, hinted to be thousands of years old and will live for thousands of years more
Their body has a special constitution aside from being extremely elemental-compatible with Teyvat’s seven elements and using multiple in one time
Their existence isn’t seen or recorded by the Ley Lines and Natlan’s Night Kingdom
As such, they’re not affected by changes within the Ley Lines and is the only one who remembers the truth of Tatarasuna and Rukkhadevata’s existence
Able to purify the poison in Dvalin’s tears
Able to purify Festering Desire that was made using Durin’s venomous remains
Able to withstand six Adeptal energies without problem
Able to take on Beisht’s assault without serious injuries
Able to withstand Xiao’s karmic debt without feeling its effects
Able to resonate with the Visions on the Statue of the Omnipresent God in Inazuma
Able to survive a slash from Raiden Shogun’s Musou no Hitotachi
Able to immediately connect with the Irminsul and maintain sanity even after coming in contact with the corruption there
Has a stronger sense of déjà vu while in the Sabzeruz samsara
Able to withstand the power of the Withering
Able to guard against Shouki no Kami’s strength with their bare hands
Able to hear Elynas’s voice and consciousness, even meeting him
Able to handle Arkhium without being contaminated
Extremely compatible with Chenyu Vale’s Spirit Veins
Their soul is colorless and untouchable/can’t be manipulated
Able to use phlogiston
Able to indwell Natlan’s Saurians
Able to purify Abyssal contamination without limit
Able to hold the Moon Marrow in their body without any issue
Was not affected by the time freeze when fighting Dottore
Has multiple titles as they travel through Teyvat
Honorary Knight of Mondstadt
Honored Guest of Liyue
Swordfish II Captain of Watatsumi Island
First Sage of Buer
Honorary Senior Researcher of the Fontaine Research Institute
Executor of Justice
‘Tumaini’, hope
Was contacted by Nicole Reeyn, one of the Hexenzirkel witches at one point, reminding them to believe what they’ve seen to understand Teyvat’s fate 

TRAVELER’S TWIN (return to top)

First to awake upon arriving in Teyvat with no memories whatsoever, and mysteriously left the spaceship without opening its doors
Listed in Irminsul as a Khaenri’ahn and not a Descender
The end of their journey’s recording was blurred by an unknown entity
The twin only arrived because the Heavens ‘responded to the summoning’. No further details given
Was imprisoned within a coffin at the Temple of Space
Lived in Khaenri’ah as their Prince/Princess for years along with The Jester who was the Royal Mage, and Dainsleif as the Twilight Sword
Their memories slowly return as time passes, including that of the Traveler
They searched the whole planet but found no trace of the Traveler
Was called a Savior when they were first granted the title of Prince/Princess by the Vinster King and the entire Court of Khaenri’ah
They said with the twin there, all pain and suffering would be washed away, and the future of Khaenri’ah would be brighter
Dainsleif calls it a cruel irony as it merely stood for the Vinster King’s lust for power
Was used as a vessel by which unlimited amount of Abyssal power could be absorbed, which then caused the Cataclysm
Was ready to do their part as the Prince/Princess and defend Khaenri’ah during the Cataclysm, but then Direidyth brought the Hibernation Chamber Key to the twin, and the twin abandoned Khaenri’ah as a result
The twin decided to see the Traveler, only to find them still sleeping with the Key on their person
The twin grabbed their sibling and ran, which led to the beginning of the game
Afterwards, they traveled with Dainsleif in search for their fate, but parted halfway for reasons unknown and now views Dainsleif as an enemy
Arrived at a place called ‘The Sea of Flowers at the End’ after they completed their journey
This is the reason why the twin can’t continue their journey between worlds with the Traveler, because the Traveler once mentioned wanting to find a place in the universe where the one flower is in full bloom
Later, they lead the Abyss Order, founded by Chlothar Alberich, to fight against destiny and to revive Khaenri’ah under a plan called Loom of Fate
It’s now completed after retrieving the eye of the first Field-Tiller from Dainsleif
They made many plans in order to fight against destiny
Tried to weaponize Dvalin by poisoning his mind but failed when the Traveler purified him
Tried to mechanized Osial’s body using the Defiled Statue and the eye of the first Field-Tiller only for Dainsleif to snatch the eye away the first time
Tried to cleanse the curse plaguing the hilichurls using the upside down fountain in the Chasm but failed when Halfdan destroyed the machine
During the events of Caribert, the twin doesn’t know how to utilize the Loom of Fate to its full potential, but believes there is still time before the Heavenly Principal awakens
Caribert died at the age of 8 during its construction, but a remnant of his consciousness remained to mature
This allows him to implant false memories for the people of Vimara Village and within Dainsleif, making him believe that he had given the eye of the first Field Tiller to the Traveler
The Loom of Fate is able to weave Ley Lines of its own at its full potential, and will lose its abilities to implant memories when that time comes
Currently, the twin managed to grow a realm based on the Khaenri’ah of 500 years ago, superimposing it over Natlan’s present-day spacetime in an Atlas of the New World, a product of the Loom of Fate
The Inteyvat was used as a base, and the twin received the Lord of the Night’s permission once she’s satisfied that it won’t have any real effect on Natlan, though she imposed an additional condition which she kept to herself
The new Khaenri’ah is enriched with memories of the old drawn from the Abyss Order and Dainsleif who was tricked into reminiscing the old Khaenri’ah
This is how the Direidyth of 500 years ago managed to meet with the Traveler, who then gave her the Hibernation Chamber Key which set off the chain reaction
The twin sees it as a mocking from Istaroth, as their actions 500 years in the future is the one that set everything in motion
The twin will one day find a suitable host location for it so Khaenri’ah can rise again

CATACLYSM (return to top)

Happened 500 years ago following the fall of Khaenri’ah’s Eclipse Dynasty
According to Xiao, the battle lasted a long time
The key was the Abyss twin who was used as a vessel by which unlimited Abyssal power could be absorbed
Unknown what exactly happened here
The people of Khaenri’ah believed that their Prince/Princess and Savior abandoned them in their time of need when that’s not the case (read: Traveler’s Twin for more)
It’s said that the sky turned red while the Cataclysm was happening
Pure-blooded Khaenri’ahn were placed with a curse of immortality and was seen as the ‘greater sinners’ by the Heavens
Half-bloods and those who used to be under the domain of the Archons were turned into monsters to roam the wilderness
Chlothar believed that the Cataclysm happened because the Gods wanted the impious people of Khaenri’ah bow their heads
Death is denied to the people of Khaenri’ah as the curse slowly erode their bodies until nothing is left, but Chlothar somehow managed to find a way to reverse it and was granted death
The five Sinners of Khaenri’ah did nothing to stop the Cataclysm as it happens, allowing the Vinster King to continue to rock the world’s foundations (see: Khaenri'ah for more)
The Seven were all summoned to Khaenri’ah except Rukkhadevata who had to protect Irminsul
Many had fought and even fell trying to quell the monsters pouring out of Khaenri’ah
Rostam the Wolf Pup bled to death, causing an inconsolable Grandmaster Arundolyn to lay down his sword
Roland the White Knight became obsessed in fighting injustice after Rostam’s death, becoming the Bloodstained Knight and sided with the Abyss
Rosalyn was stricken with grief at Rostam’s death and became the Crimson Witch
Dvalin was poisoned by Durin’s tainted blood during their fight over Dragonspine
Marchosias let his powers spread across Liyue’s soil to keep the darkness at bay, at the cost of his intelligence and strength
Chiyo Oni was devoured by a beast of sin and turned her blade against Raiden Ei
Kitsune Saiguu was devoured by the darkness. The untainted remnants of her memories coalesced into Kazari/Hanachirusato
Takamine the Mittsplitter disappeared but returned years later as an empty husk and was promptly killed by Asase Hibiki
Raiden Makoto was slain, causing tremendous grief to Ei
Greater Lord Rukkhadevata was polluted and died, but managed to create Nahida using Irminsul’s purest branch
Simurgh the divine bird became motes of power called Khvarena to purify the land
The Schwanenritter stood by the Nagarjunites at Tunigi Hollow and fought alongside them despite their ill reputation
Egeria became the Amrita, water that feeds the Harvisptokhm anchoring her consciousness to the mortal realm
Sanhaj summoned a miniature Night Kingdom and blew it up to turn the tides at the battle in Mare Jivari, both he and Tenoch lost their lives
Residents of Dunyu abandoned the area due to the Abyssal influence, turning it into a ruin
Morax dispatched his Milleliths to the Chasm when monsters overrun the area
Bosacius who had lost his sanity joined in on the fray and helped seal the Chasm bed before being trapped there together with Boyang, Yelan’s ancestor
Many of the Milleliths died protecting the area
Inazuma was engulfed in a pitch-black fog and monsters ravaged the land. Countless lives were lost and the nation barely escaped being obliterated
Sumeru was nearly drowned in a dark flood that swallowed fields, villages, and cities in what the Sages call The Black Tide
According to the Aranara, the earth witnessed the descent of the sun, evaporating all water and leaving a red world behind
One of the rifts where the monsters poured out from is the Tunigi Hollow in Sumeru
Dark fire is said to flow out from this rift together with a horde of monsters. Its remnants can be seen as gray crystals in the present day
The monsters also poured out from a crack in Wadi al-Majuj at Hadramaveth
The Sign of Apaosha, a mirror that reflects the Abyss, appeared in the sky of Temir Mountains until the waters of Amrita falls to cleanse the earth and sky
Elynas and many other sea monsters threatened the waters of Fontaine
Fontaine’s waters become contaminated as a result, creating tainted water phantasms
Natlan was overrun by Abyssal monsters for an exceedingly long time, creating a massive death toll and its people losing faith for victory or hope for the future
Two Khaenri’ahn soldiers, Zephaniah and Irizar, helped an elder from the Children of Echoes guide the Abyssal tides towards the Sulfurous Veins so it can be sealed
They refused to be seen as heroes, only believing that they were doing their duties
The Traveler later brought their souls to peace
The Night Kingdom was corrupted and the Wayob’s connection with the tribes nearly severed
It’s still in decline even after 500 years
Due to its intensity, Natlan was unable to purge the Abyss completely, only able to drive it underground
Tenoch, who was an exile, rallied heroes from the six tribes of Natlan to go against the Abyss at a place where Mare Jivari would later come to existence. Many of these heroes will later perish in the battle
The great runner Wanjiru of the Collective of Plenty and her companion, Kayeke
The brave Menilek of the Flower-Feather Clan and his companion, Ngoubou
The cunning Sanhaj Kompore of the Masters of the Night-Wind and his companion, Mahamba
He had foreseen his and Tenoch’s heroic end and the birth of Mare Jivari
The young Burkina of the Scions of the Canopy and his companion, Kongamoto
The chief of the mines, Sundjatta of the Children of Echoes and his companion, Muhuru
The giant Tupac of the People of the Springs, Tenoch’s greatest foe in his youth
The Tsaritsa had a complete personality change and cut off all ties with Barbatos for reasons unknown, changed her divine ideal from love, and froze her heart
She believes that the battle has never ended, only paused up to present day
Scaramouche says that love is just another form of sin, and she’s probably compensating for something

HEXENZIRKEL (return to top)

A mysterious organization comprised of a group of powerful women with some possessing powers that can rival a God which was founded by Alice.
The purpose, motive, and objectives of the Hexenzirkel is unclear
They sometimes conduct Irminsul exploration and possess substantial knowledge about the mysteries and true nature of the world
They once challenged the authority of the Anemo Archon Barbatos, although he managed to pacify them and bring the conflict to a peaceful resolution
Their lantern was then left in his care, which gives him the power to allow who enters the Witches’ tea party
The Witches use codenames to refer to each other
‘A’ Alice, Klee’s mother
A near-omnipotent witch with a very long life
Took in Albedo and sent him to the Knights of Favonius when Rhinedottir devoured Naberius
Able to travel between worlds
Well-versed in a variety of magical fields
Sustainer of the World’s Border. She was the one who allowed Skirk into Teyvat so long as Skirk doesn’t meddle with the world’s affairs
Currently in Nod-Krai due to the disturbances at the border, the power of the Moons being stirred, and Columbina
‘B’ Barbeloth Trismegistus, Mona’s teacher
A Visionary skilled in Astrology that she can read the world’s fortune
She created Simulanka together with Alice and ‘M’
‘N’ Nicole Reeyn
A prophetess who studies Teyvat’s direction and order, and guides others
Has a tendency to speak directly to people’s minds after giving herself a headache from talking too much initially
An Angel who witnessed the self-destruction of her species and later became human, one of the very few Angels who retained her form and power
‘R’ Rhinedottir, one of the Five Sinners of Khaenri’ah called Gold (read: Five Sinners for more)
An alchemist and researcher in Khaenri’ah whose greatest creation is Durin and Albedo. Her other creations being Rifthounds, Elynas, and Bakunawa
A transcendental being who possesses an "earth-shattering" amount of Abyssal power
She found the Heart of Naberius before devouring and merging with her, assuming her functions as Ruler of Life among the Four Shades
‘M’ Andersdotter, author of the book The Boar Princess. Full name; Anya “Marie” Andersdotter
An ordinary human mortal who has never received magic or combat training, but was able to teach Alice the meaning of grief when she passed
She created Durin within Simulanka despite Alice’s warnings that he will likely share the same fate as Teyvat’s Durin
‘J’ I. Ivanovna. N, a now-deceased Mayor of an unknown city in Snezhnaya
A member of the Hexenzirkel who left the group after her marriage, but still kept in touch with the other Witches from time-to-time
She had to end her husband when he had grown old and ill to ease his suffering
‘O’ Octavia, a wandering Witch said to have created Miliastra Wonderland
Implied that her world was destroyed and she had no courage to fight back, so she resigned herself as a wandering Witch to see other worlds
Recently, at the behest of Grand Master Varka, Barbatos set up the Tripartite Conference between himself, the Hexenzirkel and the joint forces of the Knights of Favonius
The Hexenzirkel would agree to help Mondstadt in time of need through an unknown agreement with Varka

It was said by Scaramouche that he used to be Khaenri’ah’s Eclipse Dynasty Royal Mage, but not much is known about him
