TEMPLE OF SPACE (return to top)
Also known as Segment-Domain Tau, it was created by Asmoday as a gallery to preserve fallen civilization such as those from Lang-Gan, Hyperborea, Ochkanatlan, Delphi Pytho (Enkanomiya), Remuria, Khaenri’ah, and Arcadia
The other three Shades would sometimes put whatever things they find interesting in the Temple after Asmoday’s departure
Asmoday created two stewards to act as Watchers of the Temple; to maintain order, uplifting select individuals via the Apathic Interval, and silently observing Teyvat. They uphold Asmoday’s decree that the Temple is for preservation only, with all innovation and changes forbidden
She named them Nihilita of Nothingness, and Melanta of Melancholy; the two things she could least deal with
Asmoday, Melanta, and Nihilita oversee the Temple and Teyvat within the Cage of Suchness, the highest point of the Temple. It’s separated from the rest of the Temple of Space
There is a damaged coffin in the area that shows that the Traveler’s twin once rested there
A long, long time ago, it was connected to the temple where survivors from Arcadia worshipped Istaroth
Originally, it was on the surface of Teyvat together with a temple devoted to Time, but in the wake of disaster, it was lifted to the heavens
The temple dedicated to Time remained on the surface where it eventually fell to ruin
The ruins left behind after the Temple of Space was lifted was eventually turned into a sanatorium
Not a single flutter of wind is permitted in the Temple of Space, it can only linger in the mountains, hence why the breeze at Windrest Peak is extremely strong
Humans who were picked to be preserved as living memories have to go through the Apathic Interval where they must relinquish all desires before reaching the Temple
This process is somehow aligned with the art of severing the Three Deadly Selves
After Asmoday disappeared, Ronova installed the Maranasati Caskets to guard the area
The time needed to reach a state of complete emptiness can be between a few years to several centuries
The transformation often changes the person’s personality and preventing them from obtaining Visions
When the Traveler was pulled in with Zeitlind, Paimon was mysteriously kept outside for reasons unknown
After Asmoday disappeared, Nihilita opened his eye, thus defying Asmoday’s will. He then left the Temple, abandoned his title as the Temple’s Watcher, and descended unto Teyvat looking for the right person to break the Temple free from Melanta’s tyranny
Melanta fell into madness when he realized that Asmoday will not be returning, and that Nihilita had turned against him. So he sought to overturn the changes made within the Temple, hoping it’d bring Asmoday back
He turned those in Mahavaipulya Chamber into pages, those in Luyang Academy as ink blots, and those in Desert Pavilion as sand statues. He also exiled “Maukie” before impersonating as him to capture the Traveler and Zeitlind
Humans who want to leave the Temple will have to ascend to the Cage of Suchness  via a Corridor of Nothingness born from their memories and wisdom in order to have an audience with Asmoday
In the past, the Corridor of Nothingness would never remain intact after a person has left the Temple, but after Asmoday’s departure, the rules and laws have grown weak
Melanta destroyed half the Corridor after his confrontation with Zeitlind’s sister, Raumhilda, which forced Zeitlind no choice but to give up her memories as well in order to confront him
Asmoday has never been at the Temple during its halcyon days. Only when it met its destruction did her gaze fall on it briefly
Presently, the Temple of Space had lost both its stewards; with one falling from grace and the other self-destructed after confronting Zeitlind and the Traveler, finally understanding that all creations are free after Asmoday left
