NOD-KRAI
Its other name is Nephilheim
A land made from the remnants of a fourth moon that failed to materialize
Created by the 13 Lords and Kukulkan per Xiuhcoatl’s instructions, but abandoned during the War of Vengeance/Funerary War
A land rich in kuuvahki, the most ancient and primordial form of elemental energy from the Light Realm
The kuuvahki was born when Columbina dispersed her body when she was within the Moon’s Reflection in the future, literally shaping the region
The twin travelers’ spaceship landed aeons ago on Starsand Shoal, but the ship was moved to the surface of the Frost Moon by the Heavenly Principles
A tribe indigenous of Nod-Krai is the Frostmoon Scions, also known as Moonchildren and followers of the Frost Moon, after she was released from the Moon’s Reflection by Columbina, who followed her guidance to Nod-Krai after Hyperborea was devastated by a Celestial Nail
They found refuge at a pillar created by Xiuhcoatl, which later came to be known as Sanctum of the Oathbearer where they continued their ancient rites and pray to the Frost Moon, not knowing their Goddess had died
A prophecy was later revealed, saying the Moon Goddess will one day return through the Sacred Heir born to the Frostmoon Scions
They consider the Hyperborean blood to be sacred and pure, and wants to maintain its purity in order to bear the prophesied Sacred Heir who would revive Hyperborea
This plan was called the Sacred Heir Project which uses eugenics and strict control of the family tree, but deviated at the end when Reed Miller’s mother who was a product of the project eloped with a Snezhnayan soldier instead
The Moon Maiden was born on Hiisi Island within the Court of the First Edict, foresaw and witnessed by the first Moonchanter, Aila around 500 years ago. She later sealed the court to bury the corruption and evil from the previous Archpriestess
She and her twin sister, Alia, was chosen from the lesser branch of the family to continue the Sacred Heir Project despite their bloodline not being nearly as pure
Lovia, the last Archpriestess and their grandmother, concocted a plan to raise their purity by having them kill members of the Scions and drinking their blood
Alia later escaped, leaving Aila to deal with Lovia’s obsession
She killed her corrupted grandmother under Ronova’s guide and reformed the Frostmoon Scions
The rites from Hyperborea was abolished, and Aila had her followers devoted entirely to the moonlight
Lovia was removed from historical records, and only followers with a rank of Priestess or higher are allowed to know her name
She was the one who carved the statue of the Moon Maiden depicting Kuutar’s original form
At present, the Fatui has desecrated the statues, leaving only one untouched
The Court was built by Xiuhcoatl the Pyro Sovereign based on the computerized notices when the Traveler was unsealing the area
The Verdant Crest, a Divine Tree that appeared at the birth of the Moon Maiden 500 years ago was mentioned in the Hyperborean texts long before it existed, which contradicted the Frostmoon Scion’s knowledge (a/n: kind of like how Ei said the Sacred Sakura started existing 500 years ago but Miko said it’s always been there)
Following the Cataclysm, Nod-Krai was one of the first locations to be attacked by the Abyss which they call the Wild Hunt, spearheaded by one of the Five Sinners; Rerir
They appear to be searching for something significant in the area which turned out to be Rerir’s heart that’s kept within Flins’s lantern
The Lightkeepers were founded by Solovei, the first Torchforger, who defended Nod-Krai from the Abyss
They’ve become severely weakened since
The people are able to use a power called Kuuvahki that predates the seven elements
The great thief and Treasure Hoarder’s founder Reed Miller, or Avrelian, was born here around 500 years ago
His mother was a maiden from the Frostmoon Scions, supposed to bear the Scions the prophesied heir of Elysium, but eloped with his father who was a soldier from Snezhnaya
The Khaenri’ahn mad monk Rauthvangi sent spies to capture her and brought her back to the Frostmoon Scions, leading to both her and her partner’s death
He was a childhood friend of Solovei, the founder of the Lightkeepers
He dons a silver mask after his failed execution, though no one knew why
He holds the Gods in open contempt and scorned the empty fame that the nobles are desperate for
He left five relics behind, the Proof of Cognoscenti that are claimed to lead to his greatest treasure
Founder of the Treasure Hoarders who was later publicly executed for his crimes
Alia then don his mask following his death, becoming the second ‘Reed Miller’
Brahe, Avrelian’s friend and fan, became the third ‘Reed Miller’ and spent years muddying the history of Avrelian’s Reed Miller to prevent his legacy from being corrupted
An autonomous region at the southernmost area of Snezhnaya with a harsh environment and its own rules
The Voynich Guild is the strongest force in Nasha Town, but other factions exist here as well
The Curatorium of Secrets is led by Nefer who remain steadfastly a neutral party
People from all over Teyvat gather here, congregating at Nasha Town
Vision-like objects called the Moon Wheel originate here. Its reaction is known as Lunar reactions
Originally created by the Heavenly Principles and the moon sisters to be given to the sisters’ human followers
Allows the sisters to communicate with the humans in rituals
Allows the users to manipulate the elements using a ‘purer form of power’ than the elemental energy used by regular Visions
A celebration called the Moon-Prayer Night is held when the moon is closest to Teyvat, initially started by the Frostmoon Scions to celebrate the Kuutar, it eventually became Nod-Krai’s festival
They’d wear Moon Blossoms and celebrate with songs and dance, and hand out food and handicrafts
Phantoms would rarely appear on days leading to Moon-Prayer Night due to the Abyssal power beyond Teyvat leaking through when the moon is at its closest
It became more obvious because of Dottore’s interference in creating a false moon at the border to capture Columbina and the two Moon Marrows
Supposedly the stronghold of Il Dottore, but Sandrone is the one in charge of the Fatui in the region
At some point, a large-scale development was planned for the area, but unknown what happened along the way and all traces of the development cease to exist
Now there are only scraps lying around
The remnants of the Eternal Moon fell in Nod-Krai after the Fatui managed to somehow pull it down in order to make a weapon for the Tsaritsa
One fell at Paha Island which the Fatui quickly took control of, the other at the Frostmoon Scion’s land. Lauma refuses to give them the fragment
