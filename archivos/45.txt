TENOCHTZITOC
After Och-Kan’s defeat a thousand years ago, his exiled people split into two factions; one accepted their exile and left Natlan forever, the other remained in the most barren and remote part of the nation
The group that left Natlan are the Muratans, and they arrived in Mondstadt
The group that remained created Tenochtzitoc and wielded the power of Anemo
500 years ago, it was the site of a massive battle between the Bakunawa and the people of Tenochtzitoc where the monster devoured the tribespeople, leaving Tenoch as the last survivor
It’s when Tenoch gathered the other tribes to fight against the Abyss and the Bakunawa which led to a violent explosion causing the birth of the Mare Jivari
Tenoch allowed himself to get eaten after Sanhaj performed Phlogiston Engraving on his chest and opening a miniature Night Kingdom in Bakunawa’s stomach, which then led to the explosion that shattered the Bakunawa
Around 20-30 years ago, people claimed they saw a creature the size of a mountain roaming the Mare Jivari before the area disappeared. A settlement then took over, becoming the Easybreezy Resort
In the local language, Tenochtzitoc translates to “cavern of stone and prickly pears”
But there aren’t any prickly pears growing in the modern day
The Ashas that remain in Tenochtzitoc today were souls who couldn’t return after Barbatos blew Mare Jivari out of the timeline. They settled and merged with the monetoo of Natlan
