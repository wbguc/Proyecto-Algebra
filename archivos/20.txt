KUUTAR/THE DAMSELETTE/COLUMBINA
The one and only Tri-Lune Goddess of Teyvat who wields the three lunar authorities
Born 500 years ago on Hiisi Island’s Sanctum of the Oathbearer as the new Frostmoon Goddess, witnessed by the first Moonchanter of the Frostmoon Scions, Aila
She was born because of the Luonnotar, a tiny fragment of her soul that she left in present-day Teyvat despite Teyvat’s rejection of the Frost Moon
The song that she heard the moment of her birth was sung by her future self
Initially worshipped by the Frostmoon Scions as Kuutar, successor to the original Frost Moon Huurekuutar/Canon
She left the Scions after being disillusioned by them, seeing how they worshipped her only for her to grant their wishes
Joined the Fatui after leaving Nod-Krai, becoming their Third Harbinger. But she was never given any directives from the Tsaritsa and simply roamed the gardens to sing and pass the time
She likes to join Sandrone’s tea parties much to the latter’s annoyance
Left the Fatui when she realizes that they only kept her for her power as Moon Goddess, and hid herself within the Silvermoon Hall
Dottore likes to make her move this and that with kuuvahki. She humored him for a while before distancing herself
The Tsaritsa issued the Palestar Edict, the highest of her command to retrieve Columbina at all cost
This was later rescinded after it was revealed to be Dottore’s plan in capturing Columbina to gain full control of the moons’ powers
Originally lost most of her strength the longer she remained in Teyvat, and had a strong desire to return to the Frost Moon where she belonged before she disintegrated completely
She almost did nearing Moon-Prayer Night, until she accepts the name Columbina Hyposelene that gave her a ‘reason’ to exist
After being captured by Dottore on Moon-Prayer Night, she escaped his grasp by throwing herself into the Moon’s Reflection, and started the chain-reaction that brought back the true Frost Moon within Teyvat’s fake sky and becoming the Tri-Lune Goddess
She traveled back in time within the Moon’s Reflection the more she goes forward, and is the catalyst to Nod-Krai’s entire fate
She created Luonnotar from a tiny fragment her soul, anchoring her to Teyvat
She protected Hiisi Island from Aino’s canon during Rerir’s attack
She met with the three Moon Sisters and was granted all three of their authorities
She poured her body over Nod-Krai, birthing kuuvahki on its soil and creating herself in the future
With her soul in Luonnotar as anchor, kuuvahki as her body, and her Tri-Lune authority hidden within the Frost Moon (a/n: I have no idea how this happened, it’s a plothole because the Moon’s Reflection shouldn’t have allowed it out), she’s resurrected as the new Moon Goddess in present day
Presently, she remains in the Silvermoon Hall with the kuuhenki, but is now free to roam about the lands
