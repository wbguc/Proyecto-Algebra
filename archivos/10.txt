TEYVAT (return to top)

Mentioned to be an unremarkable world at the edge of the galaxy
The Primo Elixir said that the world was in a state of intertwined order and chaos during its creation, but a ‘certain someone’ knew that this was not the case
Phlogiston is the primordial energy that courses through Teyvat
Has massive silver trees called Irminsul that grows downwards, its roots make up the Ley Line system that remembers everything that happened on Teyvat's surface and depths
The Dendro Archon's power is considered a manifestation of Irminsul, Greater Lord Rukkhadevata/Lesser Lord Kusanali is its avatar
The Ley Lines record and expel information at set times. Small minority of the Abyss order are able to control when the ley lines release information
The Sacred Sakura holds the filth of the land and purifies them, but if the filth broke out, an aggressive entity is born from it
Purified filth will appear as memories that will dissipate shortly after and absorbed back into the Ley Line network
It is shown that if its records are rewritten, it will affect everyone from Teyvat, i.e. the wipeout of Rukkhadevata and Scaramouche.
Archons are not spared from this
Those within the Temple of Space are not spared from this
Descenders are not recorded within Irminsul and are not influenced by any rewritings
Has its own laws, but these don’t affect Descenders who are alien beings that came from outside
Four Descenders have been said to arrive, but the information is likely to be manipulated as the Traveler’s twin wasn’t listed as a Descender
The Dragon King Nibelung was born alongside Teyvat
Seven Sovereign Dragons rule Teyvat alongside Nibelung, each being the strongest elemental dragon who holds the elemental Authorities. Known Sovereigns are:
Dendro Sovereign: Apep
Pyro Sovereign: Xiuhcoatl, who was able to manipulate Phlogiston. He created another dragon named Kukulkan who he calls a brother
Hydro Sovereign: Neuvillette, who was born in the vessel of a human and recently regained his Authority after the destruction of the Hydro Throne from Focalors’s death
The Primordial Sea birthed all life in Teyvat, and its original heart was the Hydro Sovereign’s
The Moon Sisters are said to have been born from this sea as well
A Voyager, named Seutervoinen, later met with Nibelung and warned him that Teyvat would be destroyed, and that he should go with her. Nibelung refused to abandon his people
She left, but promised to return
Nibelung then tried to lead the dragons to the stars by creating advanced space technologies in Chichen Uctokah and Tonatiuh (read: Ochkanatlan for more)
Nibelung later left to find the Voyager, creating three Moon Sisters to govern the cycle in his stead and giving them the power to control the laws of the world
The three moons are Aria, Sonnet, and Canon. They reside in the Lunar Palace, each circling the world in a silver chariot thrice a month
They split the power among themselves, never to consolidate it in a single body to keep the world stable
They share a love for the morning stars
If they fail to switch in time, disaster will fall the next day
Noble souls of the dead are ferried to sleep eternally in the cities upon the moon following the trails of envoys
Present day, they return to the Ley Lines and later reborn as different beings
The remnants of a failed fourth moon eventually became Nod-Krai
The creation was attempted by the Thirteen Sovereign Lords and Kukulkan per Xiuhcoatl’s instructions, but was abandoned when Xiuhcoatl joined Nibelung in the Funerary War
The three moons managed Teyvat before the arrival of Phanes and the Heavenly Principles
The first moon, Aria the Eternal Moon, gave rise to tides with her immense kuuvahki
The second moon, Canon the Frost Moon, is the brightest and became the lantern of the night
The third moon, Sonnet the Iridescent Moon, is the smallest and barely visible to the naked eye, but produces fleeting halos when she shone
Phanes/Heavenly Principle eventually arrived and fought the Seven Sovereigns for 40 years before gaining the Dragons’ Authorities and taking control of the moons, as they don’t see the Moon sisters as entities that needs to be removed
The Sovereigns descended to the bottom of the sea following their loss, and viewed the moon sisters’ following of Phanes as a betrayal
Xiuhcoatl was frozen in liquid Phlogiston and experienced a ‘false death’
The Hydro Dragon was defeated, and its heart was replaced by Egeria
The Four Shades were created by Phanes as their emissary/envoys for Teyvat
Teyvat is remade by Phanes and the Shade of Life, Naberius, for 400 years before Phanes brought forth the first humans in droves
Humanity created a covenant with the Heavenly Principles
To separate Teyvat from the rest of the universe, Phanes created the false sky
It was later revealed by Alice that Abyssal energy are all that lies beyond Teyvat’s borders, and must not make it through
Only a negligible amount passes through to influence the Verdant Crests in Nod-Krai due to the body of the Frost Moon at its closest with the planet
Angels were created by the Heavenly Principles to act as envoys and guide humanity as “flawless arbiters of justice”, teaching them language and philosophies of nature
They are commanded at birth to love all creatures of the world equally
They are described to have beautiful forms; bearing pale silver wings and wore crowns of seven colors so bright that humans couldn’t look upon them
They have a limited ability to foresee the future
They have palaces outside the realm the Gods have authority over
They condemn a parable as heresy; the god of light created a body in its own likeness, but was stolen away by the king of darkness. Thus, within that single body, powers of light and dark began to contend, creating the primordial human bearing both and possessing good and evil
The first and fairest daughter of the Heavenly Principles is named Koitar, an Angel also called Goddess of the Dawnstar. She resides deep beneath Hyperborea
Children of humanity are all who descend from the first ancestor shaped by the Primordial One. This includes the artificial people from Fontaine who were granted special pardon
The Traveler stands outside this order
Hyperborea became the Golden City which this era of humans lived in around ~6000 years ago
The heavenly envoys walked alongside humanity and dwelled amongst them
Life during this era is described as one of abundance and seemingly endless prosperity
The people learned how to use and predict lunar tides to power their magic, and established hymns known as “Runo” as its foundation
They revere not just the Frost Moon, but also the Heavenly Principles, the Four Shades, Nibelung, Koitar the first Angel, Seutervoinen, and Saarelainen the Third Descender
Other known ancient civilizations are Lang-Gan, Arcadia, and Delphi Pytho
The Voyager eventually returned and was confused at the new state of Teyvat, but doesn’t want to disturb the Heavenly Principles, so she casted her consciousness into the body of a male youth in Hyperborea
By this time, the people had angered the Heavens by asking about their fates and challenging the Gods’ authorities
He became an apostle of Hyperborea, teaching them various subjects including how to create Mekhaneikos constructs to utilize the Light Realm’s energy
The Voyager met with Koitar and told her of the forbidden origins of Teyvat and the Voyager’s identity, which then broke Koitar’s crown causing her to taste true freedom for the first time
Koitar fell in love with the youth, then told the Voyager the secrets of creation which led to the artisans of Hyperborea creating the Snowland Fae made from “fallen frostmoon light” and forged upon bones of “giant beasts”
The Voyager swore an oath to stand by the Angel’s side in rebellion against the Heavenly Principles
Their conversation didn’t go unnoticed by the three Moon Sisters
Hyperborea fell under Seutervoinen’s and Koitar’s hold for 10 years
They taught the people advanced technology
They encourage the people to explore the sea of stars beyond the false sky
They envision a time where mortals and Gods can reside in the heavens together
Nibelung returned corrupted and waged war, dubbed as War of Vengeance/Funerary Flame, against the Heavenly Principles around this time using forbidden knowledge he acquired from the Abyss outside the world believing it will help defeat his usurpers
This caused a discord amongst the Moon Sisters, with one saying they should remain with Nibelung as it was he who hung the moons. Another said taking side will bring further ruin since Nibelung returned with the Abyss
Nibelung saw their disloyalty and imprisoned the Moon Sisters within the moon’s reflection, and turned the moons as weapons against the Heavenly Principles who are likely unaware of the sisters’ imprisonment, and seeing the moons’ weaponization as an intentional breach of a peace agreement
Teyvat fell into an unprecedented state of destruction and was on the brink of collapse from the war
The Eternal Moon and Iridescent Moon shattered to pieces, while the Frost Moon is devoid of life and flung beyond the skies. All three are thus rejected by Teyvat
When the “Eternal Moon” fell, the heavens and earth were thrown into chaos. Her remains coalesced into the Moon Marrow which is kept as a sacred relic by the Frostmoon Scions, and now hidden within the Traveler’s body
When the “Iridescent Moon” shattered, the crimson shadow sank into the Abyssal sea.
When the “Frost Moon” ceased to turn, nations faltered and fell into ruin. Its new goddess, Colombina, wishes to return to it as her connection to her moon is extremely weak
The dragons celebrated their deaths, but the truth behind their ‘betrayal’ remain lost
Nibelung and the dragons’ race ended in defeat and his own thorough obliteration by the Heavenly Principles
A Shadowmoon now hangs in the sky, a projection of the real moon that became one of Zibai’s Deadly Selves’ prison
Enkanomiya sank to the bottom of the sea and was sealed there by Phanes until Orobaxi brought them back to the surface
Hyperborea was struck with a Celestial Nail and its people became homeless and destitute, and the Angels that once aided them are now gone
They mentioned a savior, who shouldn’t exist, who promised to create a new moon within the false sky and bring back their moon Goddess, but it was nothing more than a scheme by Dottore who planted ‘variables’ in the present day to gain knowledge about the power of the moons
The Angels were punished for their rebellion, with majority of them being devoured by the dragons in Natlan, leaving only Yohualtecuhtin to build Natlan’s Night Kingdom
Their previous forms, voice, and knowledge are now lost
Koitar and Seutervoinen were separated for all eternity, and their memories wiped
Nabu Malikata, Nicole Reeyn, Zibai, and the Ibis King are known surviving members of the Angels
Zibai died, but not before purging her Three Deadly Selves which Istaroth collected and saved fragments of her soul within it and sealing them away
The Heavenly Principles cast down Celestial Nails to reshape the world and purify the land from Abyssal corruption which also devastated the beings living on Teyvat
They also forbid all knowledge of the history that predated the War of Vengeance
Lang-Gan was one of the cities that were nailed, but Zibai held it back to give her people time to escape, resulting in her death
Koitar was confined to the Royal Court of the Seelie in Nod-Krai after losing her memories, weeping over her loneliness and misery with her kin she could no longer recognize
She later met with a wolf whom she finds pleasing to the eye, and named it Boreas
Boreas learned human speech and spoke with intelligence, treating others with kindness and calling her mother
She remembers her husband and their punishment by the Heavenly Principle through Boreas, and vowed to return to her homeland, awaiting for a “star beyond the stars”
The Ley Lines were modified by the Heavenly Principles to help counter the Abyss, so their stability was paramount
It’s the reason why adrift souls on the surface couldn’t be accepted by Natlan’s Night Kingdom which serves as the nation’s Ley Lines
It became a girdle that defended Teyvat from threats
The Heavenly Principles use Phlogiston as a basis to create Elemental energy to develop power to better counter the Abyss
Elemental energy is the modern counterpart of the Phlogiston, much like how light refracts into seven different colors
The War of Vengeance left the Heavenly Principles severely damaged. As a result, they couldn’t suppress the world’s original orders, which led to them collaborating with the ‘one who come after’ to use the remains of the Third Descender and create the Gnoses
The authority within it was actually taken from the Dragon Sovereigns

Winners of the war were appointed as the Seven Archons, and the Gnoses were granted to them along with a seat in Celestia
The Seven Archon seats were established 2000 years ago when the war finally ended
Unbeknownst to all, the three Moon Sisters were released from their prison after giving Columbina their Lunar Authority when Columbina traveled back in time to meet them, and she released their now-mortal bodies back to Teyvat
Aria slowly disappeared now that her existence is rejected by Teyvat with the destruction of her moon
Canon helped Hyperborea and the Frostmoon Scions after returning, but she too disappeared from Teyvat’s rejection

500 years ago, the Heavenly Principles personally intervened to stop the Abyssal corruption from Khaenri’ah, but has been asleep since then
The Sustainer of the World’s Border, Alice, allowed Skirk into Teyvat as long as she doesn’t interfere with the world’s affairs
She traveled to Teyvat in order to walk in Surtalogi’s shoes to defeat him one day
