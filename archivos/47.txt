SNEZHNAYA
Home to the Kresnik’s Torch. Every city in Snezhnaya is built around one
Its first Cryo Archon was the Belyi Tsar who had a younger brother known as the King of Summer Oak whom he loved dearly. A “tragic quarrel” led him to accidentally kill his brother
His domain became shrouded in unrelenting winter in his grief
The Snowland Fae became his extant followers, and formed the aristocracy during his reign
He was also called Emperor Monomakh before becoming the Belyi Tsar
At some point, the Belyi Tsar returned from “the ruins of the far north” and “finally opened the door the humans had once turned away”
Kitezh was the first imperial city in all of Snezhnaya
Built after the custom of the fae ancestors
It remained the center of power from its founding until the Emperor pardoned humanity and granted them membership to his realm
He then moved his seat to Snezhnograd
Eventually the Belyi Tsar began searching a way to overturn the heavenly order and create an eternal paradise, an Elysium, before his eventual death in the Cataclysm and succession by the Tsaritsa
He was influenced by the mad monk Rauthvangi who sought to rebel against the Heavenly Principles for his own reason
The fae lost their influence since his death
A nation of perpetual winter storms. Nadia said that blizzards can last for months, while Tartaglia said one can freeze just by standing around
Is far more technologically advanced than the other nations, with Alice commenting on the massive factories she saw when visiting
Dottore even created a fully-automated factory in Liyue to conduct research on Ruin Guards
The Adventurer’s Guild hailed from Snezhnaya, and each receptionist is named ‘Katheryn’, a bionic machine made by Sandrone
The remaining fae mainly live in Novokitezhgrad, Snezhnograd, and other areas in Snezhnaya, but very few in Nod-Krai due to its warmer climate
Korolevsky Troupe in Snezhnograd have regular performance, and its entry is free for Harbingers
Home to a massive diversity of fae to the point where even the faes themselves have to consult ancient books just to recognize certain names
There are 6 common fae, one of them is called a Jack-Frost, others are The Vila, Domovoy, Leshy, Volkodlak, and Snegovik
According to Viliana, Snegovik faes hate the heat and rarely leave the northern snowfields
Grand Duchess Pavlina of the House of Hesperides has completely sealed off her territory, refusing to let a single one of her subjects venture out
The less common Faes are the Lantern Fae, the Sirens, and Augury Birds
Augury Birds are able to foretell their own fate, but are also extremely small in numbers as they’re plagued by the heaviness of their foretold destiny
There’s also a near-extinct fae called the Vorcolac, said to have 2 hearts and can transform into two distinct forms; human and fae. They’re the trickiest to deal with, and it’s rumored that the Duchess, Lady Evlampiya Orlova is one
