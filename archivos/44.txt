GREAT VOLCANO OF TOLLAN (return to top)
Once housed the Sacred City of the dragons eons ago. Its other name is Land of Primal Flame
The Pyro Sovereign Xiuhcoatl was born here at the Sacred Mountain from the Primal Flame
He then gave life to his brother, Kukulkan/Waxaklahun Ubah-Kan/Sage of Stolen Flames, an offshoot of the Primal Flame
He used the flame to mold and shape the Dragonborn which later became the Saurians
The Flamelord’s Blessing is actually how he transmit light and wisdom to his kin
But due to the degradation of the Saurians, the Blessing will eventually destroy their bodies
13 artificial intelligence Sovereign Lords were created by Xiuhcoatl, each to govern an individual territory of Natlantea. There are also 3 Princes
The 13 Lords would occasionally gather in a council at the Chamber of Deliberation to vote on certain matters
The dragons would often submerge themselves in the Sea of Shifting Sentience for knowledge and wisdom from Xiuhcoatl
Xiuhcoatl fell into a false death within liquid Phlogiston when the Heavenly Principles descended, but reawakened when Nibelung returned to join the War of Vengeance
He and his kin joined in the war against the Heavens and took control of Natlan once more
The fourth moon that he had instructed the 13 Lords and Kukulkan to make was abandoned when he joined the war with Nibelung. It later became Nod-Krai
When Xiuhcoatl regained consciousness, he realized that his moment of clarity is getting shorter, so he created the Tzitzimimeh aka the Astral Assemblages within the Sacred City of Tollan, and eventually Huitzilopochtli
He modified one of his eyes to serve as a control hub for Huitzilopochtli, which became a weak point that Xbalanque used to kill him
Should Huitzilopochtli be activated, it will burn through everything, turning all of Natlan into phlogiston and destroying the Abyss using a mutual annihilation reaction via the Astral Assemblages
Waxaklahun never agreed to Xiuhcoatl’s plan and was to be impeached by the 13 Dragonlords. Many agreed to it, a few abstained from voting
The three Princes were slain in battle against humanity during the Siege of Tollan, while 10 of the 13 Lords fell when the Sacred City collapsed
The 9th Lord remained in Chichen Uctokah until the Traveler’s arrival. She then eventually died after being released from her seal
The 11th Lord who had the most contempt for humanity was split in two, with the benevolent half eventually rebuilt as Ineffa, and the malevolent half sealed until the Traveler and Ineffa destroyed her
One Lord survived to the present day, speculated to be Ajaw
The Sacred City of Tollan was sealed up and fell into darkness after Xiuhcoatl’s death, and Abyssal monsters took over
After Xiuhcoatl was slain, Waxaklahun allied with Xbalanque and, using the broken disks from his brother, created the Golden Entreaties to prevent Huitzilopochtli’s activation
At the same time, he had hoped that humanity could create a civilization even more glorious with the dragons to destroy the Abyss
His faith eventually ran out
Waxaklahun later created rumors -a lie by omission- regarding the Flamelord’s Blessing on a Saurian and how it needs to be removed by going into Tollan via his many disguises
He doesn’t deny that the Blessing can definitely destroy a modern Saurian’s body, but he never mentioned that there’s a chance he can control the Blessing
He intended to use a human to unseal the volcano via Xbalanque’s oath with him; bring the Golden Entreaties, and activate Huitzilopochtli
Present day, Waxaklahun managed to trick the Traveler and Little One into venturing all the way to Tollan using the disguise of Manqu, Titu, and Bona to activate Huitzilopochtli and turn the whole of Natlan into Phlogiston
Little One refuses and broke free from Waxaklahun’s control
After Little One’s refusal, Huitzilopochtli ran out of energy and became dormant. Waxaklahun Ubah Kan’s life essence also comes to an end
