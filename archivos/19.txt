PYRO ARCHON/HABORYM/LADY HABORYM/MAVUIKA
A human who won the battle in the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame and ascended the throne as Natlan’s Pyro Archon
She was granted knowledge and memory of Natlan by the Sacred Flame
Bears the Ancient Name ‘Kiongozi’, leader; which was once held by Xbalanque
She was born into the Scions of Canopy, and has a little sister named Hine who later grew up to be an architect
500 years ago, she placed her life within the Sacred Flame after the Cataclysm before her supposed death, all so she can return to the present and reclaim the Archon seat again
She sacrificed her power to the Sacred Flame to keep it burning when the Abyss pressed forward at one point
After failing to bring Kachina back via the Ode of Resurrection, and the failure of the old heroes’ reawakening, she suspended the Pilgrimage until further notice
The Pilgrimage is no longer in need until the foreseeable future after defeating the Abyss
Her Gnosis can be used as an emergency plan to save Natlan, but at the cost of the Night Kingdom and her people’s memories, hence why she never want to use it if possible
It will drain all souls and memories from the Night Kingdom to create a net to protect the ley lines from further corrosion which will lead to the erasure of Natlan’s history and culture
She used the power of the Shade of Death that was originally granted to Xbalanque in order to fight against the Abyss when all six heroes have reawakened
Her death is required to settle the debt
Managed to win the battle against Gosoythoth even after it mimicked the form of the Pyro Sovereign with the Traveler’s help, and purged the Night Kingdom from Abyssal influence
Had the Traveler not have an Ancient Name, she would’ve gone alone
She would’ve paid off her death debt to Ronova had Capitano not stepped in with his own plan (read: HARBINGERS for more)
Presently, she leads Natlan into a new future without the Abyss threat over their heads
