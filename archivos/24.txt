DORNMAN PORT (return to top)
A bustling port in north Mondstadt and one of the major trade hubs of Teyvat. Merchant ships of various nation enters here
Was once a fief of the Gunnhildr clan back in the aristocratic era
There’s a legend saying that Vennessa established the resistance’s first base of operations here after winning over the Gunnhildr clan
The origin of the name ‘Dornman’ is a topic up for debate
In one version, a madman wearing a crown of thorns descended Windrest Peak, rambling about the apocalypse and salvation. This theory was rejected by most researchers
In another version, the God-ruler of the land whose name had been forgotten and before being defeated by Barbatos, was the Thorn King, and his people called themselves Dornman
Other researchers say that the Thorn King mentioned in the ancient texts was an ancestor of the Gunnhildr and not a God to begin with
It was once one of Mondstadt’s frontline defenses against the Abyssal calamity 500 years ago. It took the most damage, and none of the Knights stationed here returned to the city
The graves of the Knights are hidden beneath the lighthouse which also acts as an alarm if the Abyss decided to invade
A sanatorium was created from the ruins of the Temple of Space after it was lifted to the heavens, but it was torn down some years ago
Liben says there’s a rumor saying whoever goes to the sanatorium, they’ll stay there forever
Recently, Varka ordered some Knights from his expedition, including Frederica (Jean and Barbara’s mother), to return to Dornman Port and assist in its defense
