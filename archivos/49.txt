The Chronological History of Inazuma: A Comprehensive Summary
Here is the detailed English summary of the chronological events of Inazuma, carefully adapted from the provided text to meet your specific length requirements.

The First Era and The War of Funerary Flame
The extensive historical timeline of the Inazuma region originates in the ancient epoch classified as the First Era, a formal term derived directly from the historical document known as The History of the Decline and Fall of Remuria. Approximately four hundred years following the initial appearance of the enigmatic Heavenly Principles, the geographical territory that is now recognized as Enkanomiya existed on the surface. It was merely one component of a massive, singular human civilization that unified the entire known world. During this distant and mythological time, on the landmass currently identified as Tsurumi Island, there flourished a highly unique civilization that maintained a direct, spiritual connection to Celestia, actively engaging in the worship of the Three Moons. Simultaneously, in the geographical area situated strictly to the north of modern-day Mount Yougou on Narukami Island, a distinct kingdom was meticulously constructed upon shifting sands. This kingdom was governed by a foolish and ambitious ruler whose primary objective was the construction of a towering, high garden specifically dedicated in honor of the revered white tree. Furthermore, this era witnessed the monumental birth of a magnificent and immensely powerful Electro eagle, widely recognized by subsequent generations as the Thunderbird. She originally received her true, authentic name from a mysterious female figure simply referred to as "her." Tragically, the great Thunderbird would eventually forget both the identity of this individual and the true name she had been given.

The relative tranquility of this ancient world was violently and irreversibly shattered by the devastating global conflict historically documented as the War of Funerary Flame. This catastrophic war mercilessly ravaged both the celestial heavens and the terrestrial earth. The revered Three Moons were brutally slain during the unrelenting conflict, and the subsequent, violent destruction of the Eternal Moon forcefully plunged the entire world into a state of absolute, unmitigated chaos. The ocean tides rose dramatically across the globe, culminating in a colossal, apocalyptic flood that violently swept across the territories of Inazuma. This terrifying environmental cataclysm left only a sparse, scattered collection of islands protruding safely above the turbulent water's surface. These surviving, isolated landmasses are widely believed by modern scholars to be Narukami Island, Kannazuka, Yashiori Island, Seirai Island, and Tsurumi Island. It is highly important to note that the vast majority of Watatsumi Island did not actually exist during this ancient period, as it was deliberately created much later. The catastrophic, rising floods also permanently separated the Violet Court—a sacred altar originally built to honor the Sacred Sakura—from the rest of the surviving islands, and completely obliterated the foolish sandy kingdom located north of Narukami Island, allegedly burying it deep beneath the sands forever.

The Descent of Enkanomiya and The Perpetual Mist of Tsurumi
Amidst the overwhelming, destructive chaos of the great floods, the entire region of Enkanomiya tragically fell deep into the treacherous, lightless ocean depths, effectively severing its entire population from the surface world. In their profound desperation, the trapped people fervently prayed to the Heavenly Principles and their Four Shades for divine salvation, but their desperate, echoing pleas were met with deafening silence. Left completely to their own devices in the oceanic abyss, the people were forced into a terrifying, continuous struggle for survival against the Dragonheirs of the Depths. These ferocious, predatory vishaps had previously fled into the ocean after the Heavenly Principles successfully defeated the Seven Sovereigns of the old world. Because the depths were completely devoid of natural light, the vishaps possessed an overwhelming, deadly advantage over the humans.

Simultaneously, approximately six thousand years ago, the majestic Thunderbird discovered Tsurumi Island and consciously decided to establish her permanent home there. Shortly thereafter, mysterious celestial objects—heavily speculated by scholars to be the dreaded Celestial Nails—descended violently from the heavens. One of these devastating, otherworldly objects directly struck Tsurumi Island. The catastrophic kinetic impact instantly annihilated the island's original, moon-worshipping civilization and permanently shrouded the entire landmass in an impenetrable, perpetual, and supernatural mist.

The Interim Period: Survival, Corruption, and The Kitsune Wars
During the designated Interim Period—a stretch of relative peacetime between the ancient Great War and the subsequent Archon War—the isolated people of Enkanomiya finally found a solitary beacon of hope. Istaroth, the undying wind and one of the four shining shades, became the sole divine entity who did not forsake them. The people could miraculously detect her undying wind even in the deep abyss. Empowered by divine wisdom believed to be granted directly by Istaroth, the brilliant sage Aberaku (Abrax) masterminded the construction of the Dainichi Mikoshi, which he dubbed "Helios." This monumental structure successfully generated artificial light, effectively driving away the menacing vishaps. However, when the populace attempted to return to the surface a year later, they discovered they were inexplicably magically trapped, a "power of prohibition" they attributed to the victorious Heavenly Principles. Seizing upon the people's renewed despair, a manipulative cabal of corrupt nobles deceitfully convinced the populace to worship the Dainichi Mikoshi as a literal god. They established a cruel puppet regime ruled by a selected, easily controlled child known as the "Sunchild." Righteous, brave individuals like Abrax, and subsequently Spartacus (Supada no Hiko), fiercely opposed this corrupted regime, only to be unjustly imprisoned by the manipulated Sunchildren.

Meanwhile, on the sunlit surface of Narukami Island, mischievous shape-shifting tanuki lived in absolute harmony. They were likely led by the ancient Giichiro, whose magical power could cover all of Kannazuka. This peaceful existence ended abruptly with the arrival of the kitsune, led by the Great Kitsune Hakushin, igniting a grueling one-thousand-six-hundred-year war for territorial control. The conflict finally concluded through diplomatic peace talks; although the tanuki stubbornly refused to formally admit defeat, they strategically ceded Mount Yougou and the giant Thunder Sakura to the kitsune. At an unknown point, the divine twin gods, Raiden Makoto and Raiden Ei, arrived in Inazuma from beyond the sea, manifesting atop Mount Yougou. The humans began to worship them, and the victorious kitsune willingly became their devoted servants, constructing the magnificent Grand Narukami Shrine in their honor.

The Archon War and The Salvation of Enkanomiya
The Archon War, an era of relentless warfare triggered when seven divine seats in Celestia officially opened approximately 3,700 years ago, plunged Teyvat into a bitter, bloody struggle for absolute supremacy. Raiden Ei valiantly conquered numerous formidable youkai races, successfully unifying the scattered tribes and bringing all of Inazuma’s distinct islands under her absolute rule, raising majestic cities like Araumi, Hiraumi, and Mumei. Believing her sister Makoto was fundamentally better suited for complex leadership, Ei willingly sacrificed her physical body so Makoto could safely ascend to Celestia as the first Electro Archon, the God of Eternity. Makoto then miraculously reforged Ei's body, allowing Ei to serve secretly as her shadow double (kagemusha).

Concurrently, the magical seal isolating Enkanomiya weakened, permitting the arrival of Orobashi, a massive serpent god fleeing the brutal realities of the Archon War. Finding a desperate child who begged him to be their god, Orobashi heroically overthrew the tyrannical Sunchildren, completely freed the subjugated populace, and protected them from the deep-sea dragons. He bolstered the Dainichi Mikoshi's light, supervised the research of the Bathysmal Vishaps, and established cultural practices to prepare his people for the surface. Fulfilling a profound promise, Orobashi miraculously created Watatsumi Island entirely out of the massive branches of coral growing directly from his own divine body, finally allowing his devoted followers to live comfortably above the surface as part of the Inazuma archipelago. For years, they lived peacefully, calling their new home Sangonomiya and renaming their old home Enkanomiya.

The Tragic Invasion of Yashiori Island and Post-War Sorrow
The fragile peace was ultimately shattered. Following years of severe famine, Orobashi's desperate people urgently pleaded with him to authorize an aggressive invasion of the fertile Yashiori Island. Although initially hesitant due to his fear of Narukami’s power, Orobashi yielded, viewing this doomed conflict as a way to fulfill his divine sentence from Celestia. The forces of Watatsumi successfully took control of the ancient city of Mumei. When news reached Narukami Island, a devastating war erupted. During the brutal conflict, the Watatsumi warrior Akuou slew the Great Tengu Sasayuri, a cherished friend of Ei. Thrown into a state of absolute blinding rage, Ei unleashed the apocalyptic Musou no Hitotachi, instantly annihilating Orobashi and Akuou, and cleanly splitting Yashiori Island entirely in two. The resulting war was incredibly devastating; Mumei was destroyed, and the sea turned a horrific crimson. Orobashi's lingering, toxic resentment birthed the dangerous Tatarigami, necessitating the creation of Narukami Pillars to violently suppress his spiritual corruption. With the Shogunate securing an absolute victory, Watatsumi Island officially fell under the total dominion of the Electro Archon, effectively concluding the Archon War in Inazuma.

Tragedies continued in the post-war era. Around 1,500 years ago on Tsurumi Island, a young boy named Ruu befriended the Thunderbird, naming her "Kanna Kapatcir." Misunderstanding the situation, the desperate islanders tragically sacrificed Ruu to please the god. Enraged by the brutal murder of her innocent friend, the Thunderbird instantly annihilated the entire tribe and cursed the island to continuously relive its day of destruction. She fled to Seirai Island, wreaking immense havoc until Raiden Ei hunted and killed her as a dangerous monster. The Thunderbird's lingering hatred manifested as the violent Thunder Manifestation, requiring the immediate construction of the Asase Shrine for suppression.

The Dark Cataclysm and The Fall of Khaenri'ah
Exactly 500 years ago, Inazuma faced its greatest existential threat during the apocalyptic event universally known as the Cataclysm. Horrific, corrupted monsters from the Abyss violently invaded the aboveground world of Teyvat, causing widespread, unimaginable devastation. The people attributed the creation of these dark monsters to "Gold" Rhinedottir, one of the Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah. The Kingdom of Khaenri'ah was subsequently obliterated entirely by the Heavenly Principles, triggering a devastating ripple effect.

During the Cataclysm, Makoto secretly left Ei behind to defend Inazuma and traveled alone to Khaenri'ah, where she was tragically slain in the chaotic conflict. When Ei finally arrived, she found her sister dying in her arms. Ei managed to miraculously save Makoto's realm of consciousness, safely bringing it back to Inazuma. Upon her return, Ei discovered that the Sacred Sakura had suddenly and inexplicably appeared at the peak of Mount Yougou—an incredible, paradoxical phenomenon resulting from the magic surrounding Makoto's consciousness and the temporal manipulations of Istaroth.

The defense of Inazuma cost many beloved figures their lives or sanity. The renowned swordsman Takamine the Mistsplitter was overwhelmed by Abyssal monsters and vanished. The mighty oni warrior Mikoshi Chiyo was swallowed whole by a beast of sin; although she survived, the abyssal filth deeply corrupted her, driving her to madly betray Ei and forcing her tragic exile. Furthermore, the beloved Kitsune Saiguu was entirely consumed by the dark forces, her pure memories dissolving completely into filth. Ultimately, utilizing the immense, cleansing power of the newly manifested Sacred Sakura, Ei managed to successfully overcome the invading monsters and quell the disaster, forging the isolated eternity of modern Inazuma.
