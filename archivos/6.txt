THE HARBINGERS
Despite being a Khaenri’ahn survivor and disliking the Gods, Pierro sided with the Tsaritsa for an unknown reason
It was said by Scaramouche that he used to be Khaenri’ah’s Eclipse Dynasty Royal Mage, but not much is known about him
He seems to want something from Scaramouche, as he sends him to the Abyss constantly
His plan is to uphold the virtue of the folly, and to ensure that the Tsaritsa will once again be a God who loves all humans and to have all their wishes be granted
He and the Rooster are leading the charge for Project Stuzha

Presumably born several decades after the Cataclysm, and for unknown reason chased out of his hometown with pitchforks and clubs
His name is Zandik, and he was a student of the Akademiya but was kicked out before he could graduate. He openly proclaimed that humans are nothing more than a machine with certain level of complexities, and could be modified to be enhanced
His studies frequently brought him to the desert, where he met Thoth at one point, who distrusted Zandik’s narcissistic and self-serving worldview
At one point, he murdered his fellow scholar named Sohreh after “acting out of authorization for the third time”. He disappeared some time later
Pierro personally recruited him into the Harbingers
He was sent by Pierro to sow discord in Inazuma by pretending to be a Fontainean engineer named Escher who then sabotaged the Mikage Furnace ~400 years ago and killed Niwa
He experimented on Scaramouche for decades and managed to create independent individual segments representing himself at different ages in order to preserve their perspectives
He viewed this as his crowning achievement as a researcher
Later, those segments were destroyed under Nahida’s orders
He once experimented on Eleazar patients in Dar al-Shifa hospital before it was abandoned. Collei was one of his experiments
He has goals in weaponizing dreams and memories after hearing tales of the Aranara
Has the knowledge to manipulate the Akasha by sending hallucinations to the people of Sumeru and sealing Nahida’s consciousness
He worked together with the Sages to create a God out of Scaramouche, and leaves once his business is completed without caring for his experiment
Managed to obtain both Dendro and Electro Gnoses from Nahida after exchanging knowledge with her
The Damselette is Nod-Krai’s Moon Maiden, Kuutar. She has left the Fatui and resided in the Silvermoon Hall away from everyone else, and now she’s the new one and only Tri-Lune Moon Goddess of Teyvat (read: Columbina for more)
She’s said to be oblivious and innocent at any given time, and unconcerned and unfeeling in any given situation by Scaramouche. Even Childe is wary of her
She was never given any orders to mobilize in the Fatui by the Tsaritsa, so she spent her days singing in the garden or enjoying Sandrone’s tea parties
She enjoyed spending time with Signora, Sandrone, and Arlecchino
She shaped Nod-Krai’s fate during her battle with Dottore
La Signora is slain by Raiden Shogun at Tenshukaku after losing the Duel Before the Throne against the Traveler
Her name was originally Rosalyne, a student of Sumeru Akademiya prior to the Cataclysm 500 years ago
She fell in love with Rostam at Ludi Harpastum, and was aggrieved when he died
She used her knowledge to turn herself into the Crimson Witch and burnt everything in her path
She gets mad whenever someone calls her a witch
Pierro recruited her and gave her a Cryo Delusion to temper her flames
She managed to retrieve both the Anemo and Geo Gnosis for the Tsaritsa
Scaramouche, a humanoid puppet, decided to wander Sumeru after erasing his existence from Irminsul before regaining his true past from Nahida. He was then enrolled in the Akademiya as a Driyosh of Vahumana. Went to Nod-Krai to stop Dottore, unknown where he is now
He was built by Ei from the branches of Irminsul using technology left by the Khaenri’ahn schools of mechanized engineering
She did this to store her Gnosis, but his gentle disposition made her free him, though he saw it as an abandonment and betrayal
He was found by Katsuragi within Shakkei Pavilion, and was taught how to live like a human while living in Tatarasuna. He learned smithing from Niwa, an Isshin Art master
He believed he was betrayed a second time when Niwa disappeared after the Mikage Furnace failed, not knowing he was killed by Dottore
He then met a sickly child after leaving Tatarasuna, only for the child to die after one night. He saw this as the final betrayal
He joined the Fatui after being persuaded by Pierro, and Dottore released the power that Ei had sealed within him, making him stronger than his original construct
After exchanging the Traveler’s life with Ei’s Gnosis from Yae Miko, he went off-radar before arriving in Sumeru and ascended as Shouki no Kami
He spent some time within the Sanctuary of Surasthana after being defeated and the Electro Gnosis ripped out of him before being sent by Nahida to retrieve information from Irminsul with the Traveler
The truth about Niwa and the Tatarasuna incident came to light, and he wiped himself from history much like Rukkhadevata did
The Traveler later found him as a completely innocent puppet, he implored to see through the hidden records Nahida managed to save in time and accepted his past transgressions
After completing his mission in Liyue, Childe traveled around before being trialed in Fontaine and ended up fighting the All-Devouring Narwhal in the Primordial Sea. He’s returned to Snezhnaya by Pierro’s order for Project Stuzha
His real name is Ajax, and he was originally a quiet, meek child until he fell into the Abyss at age 14
He was trained by an Abyssal swordswoman named Skirk for 3 months. When he re-emerged, only 3 days had passed on Teyvat
He became bloodthirsty after that, and his father enlisted him into the Fatui hoping to tame him, only to see him take down a group of full grown men
Pulcinella recruited him as a pretense to mete out punishment, but made him the youngest Harbinger
He dislikes covert operations, in extend to that, he dislikes a majority of the Harbingers
When he was in Fontaine, he lost control over his Vision due to the Narwhal’s influence and was sent to the Fortress of Meropide after his trial before fighting said Narwhal
Returned to Fontaine temporarily to find Skirk, but eventually had to return to Snezhnaya
Unknown where Pantalone is presently situated, but he had fully rented out the Goth Grand Hotel for time indeterminate and paid in full
He is the owner of the Northland Bank obsessed with fair exchange to the point of wanting to overthrow the imbalance between Gods and humans
He works closely with Dottore who relies on his financial support in order to continue his research and experiments

Arlecchino is the head of the House of the Hearth, an intelligence-gathering orphanage based in Fontaine.
She has the mysterious power of Khaenri’ah’s Balemoon bloodline that she herself isn’t too knowledgeable about
She does know that if she overuse her power, it will corrupt her, and will turn her into a completely different person if it reaches her heart
She became the head after killing the previous head, Harbinger Crucabena, and was pardoned of her crimes by the Tsaritsa
Prior to the flooding, she nearly assassinated Furina to obtain her Gnosis, but realized that Furina doesn’t have it and seemed to be under a curse
The Hydro Gnosis was later given by Neuvillette as a diplomatic exchange for her contributions in helping Poisson
If any of the orphans wanted to leave, they first have to fight her to earn their freedom before ingesting flames extracted by her to wipe their memories of the House. She made an agreement with Neuvillette that these children were not to be trialed for their past once they’ve left
One of the orphans faked her death and took the place of Shuumatsuban Gendo Ringo at the Grand Narukami Shrine (a/n: there’s a whole secret quest for this)
Scaramouche says she’s a wolf in a sheep’s clothing, putting a cordial front to exert control over people
This, however, was due to a biased accounts of her past spread by Pulcinella whom Arlecchino mutually dislikes
She’s now holding the Crimson Moon marrow which Columbina entrusted to her after Dottore’s defeat
The Captain was a survivor, former commander, and Sentinel Knight from Khaenri’ah named Thrain. He’s now eternally seated upon the Throne of the Primal Fire after merging his existence with the Lord of the Night to strengthen her existence, and to send the souls of his Khaenri’ahn brethren to death
An honorable, upstanding man in the eyes of many. Scaramouche calls his righteousness an annoying trait
Considered to be the strongest human
Has a heart that was modified by Khaenri’ahn technology to convert memories and souls from the Ley Lines into knowledge
After the Cataclysm, he used his heart as a vessel to store souls of his fallen Khaenri’ahns
Was cursed with immortality after the events of the Cataclysm, which led him to resent the Gods and Ronova
He had no idea about the Five Sinners’ exploitation of Abyssal Power, and finds that the Gods’ collective punishment of immortality to be abhorrent
He craves for death, and he wants to grant that same end for his fellow innocent Khaenri’ahns (read: KHAENRI’AH for more)
He promised his fellow Khaenri’ahns that he will grant them salvation, which brought him to Natlan due to the unique Ley Line structure that is disconnected from other nations
He tried to manipulate the Ley Lines in other nations, but all ended in failure

Ayizu also told him the secrets of the Gnosis before his death, though unsure how he knew about it
Due to his rotting flesh, he continuously wears a mask

Alain equipped her with an emotion module which allowed her to learn and process feelings. Later, he gave her Pulonia after her learning capabilities exceeded expectations

Unknown what her work was
