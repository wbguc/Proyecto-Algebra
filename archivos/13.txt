THE PRIMORDIAL ONE/PHANES (return to top)

Primordial God of Teyvat, said to have come from another world as the First Throne
It’s said they tread the morning stars
Was born from an egg, androgynous in nature, has wings and a crown
Fought the seven Vishap Lords for 40 years together with its four shining Shades who don’t like to be mentioned by name by any living being
Shade of Time: Istaroth, a friend of Barbatos who granted him fragments of her powers and authority, and a forgotten figure in Mondstadt’s history, also a God of Enkanomiya. She ensured that the twin’s actions set the motion for the events 500 years ago
Shade of Life: Naberius, she created all life on Teyvat, including Egeria the Hydro Archon before being devoured by ‘Gold’ Rhinedottir after the fall of Khaenri’ah
Shade of Death: Ronova, who helped establish Natlan’s Night Kingdom, lent Xbalanque her powers (she will deny any involvement) and fell into ‘self-pity’ after overstepping her authority as a Shade and displeasing the Heavenly Principles.
Shade of Space: Asmoday, the Unknown Goddess who stopped the twin travelers. She’s currently missing and tainting the spaces she’s at. Ronova believes she ‘loves her new Master more than she loves herself’.
Recreated the world for 400 years after defeating the Seven Sovereigns
For the world to be made, the egg’s shell had to be broken. Phanes instead used it to separate the universe and the microcosm of the world, creating the fake sky
Phanes created the heaven and earth for the sake of humanity
Holds humans in high esteem and has a sacred plan for them, but forbids them from falling to temptations
If the humans are happy, they too are happy
Humans appear as numerous as the stars in the sky after Phanes made them
Phanes and the humans forged a covenant together before entering a new age together
Humans worked the world together with the heavens, prosperity and riches were in abundance
During the War of Vengeance with the dragons, Phanes and three of their shades didn’t hear the laments of Enkanomiya, only Istaroth did
Phanes won the war and defeated Nibelung and the dragons, but sustained massive damage as a result
They placed a ban from letting anyone from Enkanomiya returning to the surface a year after the creation of the Hyperion
Phanes had their major functions severely damaged, so they came together with the ‘one who come after’ to create the Gnoses using the Third Descender’s (aka Saarelainen) body to suppress the world’s original order after Saarelainen opened the gates of Pohjola in order to marry the daughter of Pakkaisukko (a/n: exact details are yet to be revealed)
This later led to the Archon War, where the Seven Archons rule Teyvat in Phanes’s stead
The daughter of Pakkaisukko would never again be wed
Dropped multiple heavenly nails; one on Dragonspine, the other into the Chasm, another in Tsurumi, another implied to be somewhere in the desert, and another in the Night Kingdom of Natlan
It is said by Sumeru’s Goddess of Flowers that Phanes brought down heavenly nails to destroy the land after Nibelung brought ‘illusions that could break through shackles to the land’
According to the Goddess’s words, the master of the heavens was “consumed by fear for the rising tide of delusion and breakthroughs”
The nails have the ability to purify and stabilize powers that shouldn’t exist in Teyvat, changing its shape such as Oozing Filth in the Chasm
After Orobashi brought everyone to the surface, there are no records on what happened to Phanes
The Goddess of Flowers imply that Phanes shouldn’t be sought after, and asking it questions about the sky and the Abyss is forbidden least calamity falls
