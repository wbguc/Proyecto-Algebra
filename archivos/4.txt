HEXENZIRKEL (return to top)

A mysterious organization comprised of a group of powerful women with some possessing powers that can rival a God which was founded by Alice.
The purpose, motive, and objectives of the Hexenzirkel is unclear
They sometimes conduct Irminsul exploration and possess substantial knowledge about the mysteries and true nature of the world
They once challenged the authority of the Anemo Archon Barbatos, although he managed to pacify them and bring the conflict to a peaceful resolution
Their lantern was then left in his care, which gives him the power to allow who enters the Witches’ tea party
The Witches use codenames to refer to each other
‘A’ Alice, Klee’s mother
A near-omnipotent witch with a very long life
Took in Albedo and sent him to the Knights of Favonius when Rhinedottir devoured Naberius
Able to travel between worlds
Well-versed in a variety of magical fields
Sustainer of the World’s Border. She was the one who allowed Skirk into Teyvat so long as Skirk doesn’t meddle with the world’s affairs
Currently in Nod-Krai due to the disturbances at the border, the power of the Moons being stirred, and Columbina
‘B’ Barbeloth Trismegistus, Mona’s teacher
A Visionary skilled in Astrology that she can read the world’s fortune
She created Simulanka together with Alice and ‘M’
‘N’ Nicole Reeyn
A prophetess who studies Teyvat’s direction and order, and guides others
Has a tendency to speak directly to people’s minds after giving herself a headache from talking too much initially
An Angel who witnessed the self-destruction of her species and later became human, one of the very few Angels who retained her form and power
‘R’ Rhinedottir, one of the Five Sinners of Khaenri’ah called Gold (read: Five Sinners for more)
An alchemist and researcher in Khaenri’ah whose greatest creation is Durin and Albedo. Her other creations being Rifthounds, Elynas, and Bakunawa
A transcendental being who possesses an "earth-shattering" amount of Abyssal power
She found the Heart of Naberius before devouring and merging with her, assuming her functions as Ruler of Life among the Four Shades
‘M’ Andersdotter, author of the book The Boar Princess. Full name; Anya “Marie” Andersdotter
An ordinary human mortal who has never received magic or combat training, but was able to teach Alice the meaning of grief when she passed
She created Durin within Simulanka despite Alice’s warnings that he will likely share the same fate as Teyvat’s Durin
‘J’ I. Ivanovna. N, a now-deceased Mayor of an unknown city in Snezhnaya
A member of the Hexenzirkel who left the group after her marriage, but still kept in touch with the other Witches from time-to-time
She had to end her husband when he had grown old and ill to ease his suffering
‘O’ Octavia, a wandering Witch said to have created Miliastra Wonderland
Implied that her world was destroyed and she had no courage to fight back, so she resigned herself as a wandering Witch to see other worlds
Recently, at the behest of Grand Master Varka, Barbatos set up the Tripartite Conference between himself, the Hexenzirkel and the joint forces of the Knights of Favonius
The Hexenzirkel would agree to help Mondstadt in time of need through an unknown agreement with Varka
