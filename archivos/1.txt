THE TRAVELER (return to top)

Currently traveling through Teyvat to find the truth of the world, and to reunite with their twin
Described as the ‘Child of Dandelions’ by the Aranara
Liloupar knows that they’re an exiled Prince/Princess
Ei found out that they’re a star after being told
The two Watchers of Temple of Space seem to know the Traveler
Melanta says the Traveler is not the child of humanity
Nihilita says they’re a “returnee from the sea of stars”
The last descendant of a subjugated civilization that forced the twins to travel in their spaceship to find a new world with “potential” to rebuild their civilization
The ship’s maintenance, course, and choice of destination was left under the control of an entity speculated to be an extremely sophisticated A.I.
It traveled for thousands of years while the twins hibernated within the Hibernation Chamber
The ship is a pioneer vessel, capable of terraforming an entire planet; modifying its lithosphere, biosphere, and atmosphere, and equipped with a genetic vault
It flew past Teyvat thousands of years ago, but didn’t land
As long as their attitude of kindness and nobleness remain, they will always have companions
Crash-landed in Nod-Krai eons ago as the Fourth Descender, and remained asleep within the Hibernation Chamber for an unknown amount of time
Awakened by their twin 500 years ago despite the doors never being opened
Their powers sealed after battling against the Shade of Space; Asmoday, when they wanted to leave Teyvat, and was put to sleep again for another 500 years
Befriended a fairy named Paimon 2 months before the start of the game
Paimon was fished out by the Traveler after she got exhausted one day and was caught in a strange whirlpool before being sucked out to sea
Not much of a talker and prefers to keep things bottled up
A lot older than they look, hinted to be thousands of years old and will live for thousands of years more
Their body has a special constitution aside from being extremely elemental-compatible with Teyvat’s seven elements and using multiple in one time
Their existence isn’t seen or recorded by the Ley Lines and Natlan’s Night Kingdom
As such, they’re not affected by changes within the Ley Lines and is the only one who remembers the truth of Tatarasuna and Rukkhadevata’s existence
Able to purify the poison in Dvalin’s tears
Able to purify Festering Desire that was made using Durin’s venomous remains
Able to withstand six Adeptal energies without problem
Able to take on Beisht’s assault without serious injuries

Able to withstand the power of the Withering
Able to guard against Shouki no Kami’s strength with their bare hands
Able to hear Elynas’s voice and consciousness, even meeting him
