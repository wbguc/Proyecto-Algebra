THE FIVE SINNERS (return to top)

Five individuals with great esteem and were considered the best of their peers
They have the power to stop King Irmin from rocking the consciousness of Teyvat, but did nothing, none of them defended Khaenri’ah
They instead stole one-sixth of the “world-shattering” amount of Abyssal power that the King had the Traveler’s Twin absorb, becoming sinners and transcendent beings
“The Wise” Hroptatyr
President of the Universitas Magistrorum, and Chief Royal Mage by royal appointment, one of the most trusted by the King
Wielder of the claymore; Starkuthr that holds the power of Reason
“The Visionary” Vedrfolnir, Dainsleif’s brother
A prophet whose prophecies are never wrong, and one of the most trusted by the King
Aware of the relationship between Rerir and Tholindis, but told Rerir to find someone else as their marriage will not last nor end happily
Warned the King that Khaenri’ah will fall if he continues his path
This caused the King to blind both his eyes and threw him in a cell, prompting Dainsleif to rescue him which kickstarted the Cataclysm
Gave Dainsleif a special ring that continues to reset his condition, enabling him to preserve his memories despite the Curse of Immortality
Appeared to Chlothar and the Traveler (who was going through their twin’s memories) as a crystal in chains, urging them to become transcendent beings as he knew of their fate
He disappeared when the Traveler’s Twin returned to the same spot shortly after
The stone slates outlining Fontaine’s prophecy is hinted to be his doing
Wielder of the catalyst; Gleipnir that holds the power of Time
“Gold” Rhinedottir
Practitioner of the Art of Khemia and architect of the Primordial Human Project, she created a myriad of creatures which cause her to develop a penchant for madness
Riftwolves from alfisol, Durin from humus, and Albedo from chalk, though his failed predecessor known as Subject Two was disposed in Durin’s stomach
She also created Bakunawa using a transcendental Abyssal power, but lost control, causing the creature to swallow parts of her body
She called the ‘unknown form’ of Eynas from the ‘cosmic darkness’, promising him of a beautiful world, and a strong healthy body
She later found the Heart of Naberius and merged with her, assuming the functions as the Shade of Life
She left Albedo, whom she had taught everything after creating him post-Cataclysm, in Alice’s hands shortly after
Wielder of the sword; Logseims, that holds the power of Life
“The Foul” Surtalogi
Born in a prison to criminals sentenced to death. He spent his childhood wandering the streets and facing criticisms
Student of a teacher named Haden together with Dainsleif, they both learned the Black Serpent Bladework
He aspired to follow the code of knighthood, but was punished by Haden who sought to retain his prestige, resulting in the loss of various limbs
At some point in his youth, he stole forbidden knowledge to cure his condition
Master of the All-Devouring Narwhal which he regarded as a pet, but unknown when he obtained the creature
After becoming a Sinner, he left Teyvat as he had lost interest in the world, seeking stronger opponents in the universe
He adds his own strength by devouring his fallen adversaries
He found Skirk after her world was destroyed, and took her in as a disciple after remembering Vedrfolnir’s prophecy. He later left her, telling her that he will eventually destroy all that she held dear
He taught her how to wield the power of the Abyss, including the Seven-Shifting Serpent, a technique from Khaenri’ahn Universitas Magistrorum that allows her to mimic the elemental energy of Teyvat
At some point, he told Skirk to find Seutervoinen, the Apostle of the Far North who was also a voyager from beyond Teyvat
Wielder of the bow; Gusisnautar, that holds the power of Void
“Rächer of Solnari” Rerir
He was originally a normal man serving as an intelligence officer under King Irmin, assigned in secret to wipe out the remaining Crimson Moon Dynasty’s descendants
He has a girlfriend named Tholindis, whom he proposed but have yet to receive an answer
He didn’t realize she was one of the Crimson Moon descendants until her file appeared on his desk as his next target. He refused to kill anymore
He was tasked to free Vedrfolnir from his prison cell when King Irmin eventually lost his mind, and Dainsleif storming the palace
Tholindis eventually jumped into a portal to another world when monsters eventually attacked the Universitas Magistrorum where she worked. This left Rerir in despair
She had known about his secret work, and promised to restore him to his normal life
He joined the Sinners, hoping to use the Abyssal power to bring Tholindis back, but his body was torn apart when he reopened the moon gate to find her
The fragments end up scattered all over Nod-Krai, which later became the Wild Hunt
The Wild Hunt, at his command, began to plot and restore his physical form
He became unnaturally obsessed and fixated with the moons beyond the false sky as a result
He would also absorb kuuvahki to restore his Abyssal power, even deceiving the Traveler by shapeshifting as their twin into stealing the Moon Marrow that has immense kuuvahki energy
He continued to wander Nod-Krai, absorbing kuuvahki after the failed attempt with Nasha Town’s kuuvahki cannon
He was later sealed beyond Teyvat’s borders after Columbina restrained him, using his own power against him, and Dainsleif keeping the moongate open
He remained imprisoned within the Moon’s Reflection together with Tholindis’s soul
Wielder of the polearm; Barnstokkr, that holds the power of Death
