OCHKANATLAN
Waxaklahun calls it a city that can rival Tollan before the false sky enveloped the world. Meaning it has existed before the arrival of the Heavenly Principles
The dragons built it to find other worlds after Nibelung was visited by the Voyager, so they set up a cable to reach beyond the skies
They were prepared to leave it beyond the world’s orbit and use it to tighten the sky-cable
Nibelung might’ve spearheaded the whole operation before leaving Teyvat
The voices of the Wayob can’t be heard here, nor do any mystical arts of the Masters of the Night-Wind work for some reason
Was rebuilt by the followers of Xbalanque before it was later abandoned
Before its existence however, it was called Chichen Uctokah, also known as the Land of the Seven Flames, and it was ruled by the Dragonlord Ix Uxul Tz'ib Bolon Ch'ule-L, or Ixlel for short
Och-Kan later trapped her within the old core of Chu’ulel and stole her power to build Ochkanatlan’s new core
Adventurers who arrived in Ochkanatlan noticed how the scale of the city seem to dwarf the size of an average human, and concluded that Och-Kan ‘piggybacked’ the ancient city to create Ochkanatlan
Prior to becoming its King, Och-Kan was imprisoned by the dragons before being saved by Xbalanque. Och-Kan then devoted his life to serve Xbalanque
Och-Kan is the son of the Sage Waxaklahun Ubah Kan who has the soul of a human. He was tasked to slay the decrepit Xiuhcoatl before taking its place but he ended up betraying his father and joined the human tribes instead
In his blood flows the Flamelord’s Blessing
In order to hide his draconic self, Och-Kan hides his face behind a mask
Och-Kan knew about his father’s plan in recreating Natlan with a new human-dragon civilization, hence his betrayal
He also tricked Yupanqui into a mire, humiliating him into joining them
Ixlel once whispered to Och-Kan that he was the fated Sovereign which he found to be nauseating, and further fuels his hatred for the dragons
She showed him all there is to know about the ancient empire without holding back; the ladder up the firmament, the things that fell from the three moons, and many others
This led him to imprison her, and stole her powers to form a new core of Chu’ulel that will only serve humanity
After Xbalanque ascended as the Pyro Archon, Och-Kan took the reins of authority and proclaims himself to be the Sacred Lord of Ochkanatlan, ruling the city with an iron fist to ensure humanity’s survival
He believed that Xbalanque should remain as the one and only God of Natlan when the people were waiting for his successor
He broke Xbalanque’s commandment with the Sage to have dragons and humans coexist together by establishing a city where heaven and earth were separated
He persecuted the dragons in his rule, along with anyone who dare defy him
The hatred he has against the dragons cause concern among his peers
He banned the practice of live sacrifice and the worship of the Night Kingdom, weakening the people’s connection with the Wayob
He used the Turnfire to oppress those who oppose him
Using the cables left by the dragons, Och-Kan built a sky-faring ship known as the Tonatiuh, also called the Skyserpent Ship, that is rumored to be full of treasures
The ship is actually used to house the Sage’s Golden Entreaty which is said to be able to cleanse Abyssal powers, but it also comes with equal amount of danger
The Golden Entreaty is actually a part of a pact between the Sage and Xbalanque for when the world plunges into darkness: Piece their Golden Entreaties together, and activate Huitzilopochtli (read: Great Volcano of Tollan)
The rumor was started by the Sage who was bitter over his son’s betrayal, and his desire to get his Golden Entreaty back which he couldn’t touch due to his pact with Xbalanque
Later, Och-Kan’s behavior grew increasingly erratic when he saw that the pupils of his eyes slowly becoming vertical
This led him to order craftsmen to excavate gigantic beastly constructs from underground
The Flamelord’s Blessing in his blood allowed him to discover the secrets of the dragons, in which he used against his war with the remaining dragons in Natlan
The Sage later come to scold Och-Kan for killing his dragon brethren, but Och-Kan argued that it’s for the betterment of humanity
The Sage said that due to Och-Kan’s madness, he had opened the doors to malevolent forces and betrayed the oath between Xbalanque and the Sage
The Sage promised to retrieve Och-Kan’s soul as part of his experiment
To protect his people, Och-Kan split his body and mind before being used by “that person”, unknown who, as an Abyss conduit
His mind is sealed into the new core of Chu’ulel, while his body remain fighting alongside the humans until it was eventually swallowed by the Abyss
Because of the Abyssal contamination, scattered notes around Ochkanatlan suggests that the humans become zombie-like creatures
Eventually, the Scarlet-eyed Hero who was the second Pyro Archon and his entourage which includes the now-elderly Yupanqui, successfully overthrew Och-Kan who then fell from a great height
Yupanqui stole the Turnfire from Och-Kan and used it against his army before being burned by the same fire not long after
He turned around when he believed Och-Kan was calling for him, which led him to his death
Yupanqui is later known as Firethief for his brazen act
The Scarlet-eyed Hero offered his own heart to emulate Xbalanque and confront his own demise, as self-sacrifice is seen as an honor for the victor
His sacrifice received the response it deserved and he returned to the Flame
Twins Ahpub and Ixquieh died in battle one after another, while the artisan Manqu wishes for his name to be wiped from history after the battle. Sakkuk lived to her old age with a love of jewelleries in remembrance of Manqu
Och-Kan survived the battle, but due to Abyssal corruption, his body became the feared evil dragon while his mind remained within the core of Chu’ulel until Bona awakened it a few hundred years ago
Bona later died of unknown means, and Och-Kan regretted not being able to save her
The Sage constructed a legend about the Jade of Return as an experiment in his understanding of humans by traversing the lands using the many masks he has, thanks to Och-Kan devouring countless souls
He wants to understand humans in order to save his own race, and because he knows humans like to chase over things that don’t exist
This experiment was born because of Och-Kan’s betrayal, as the Sage believed that he has much to learn about humanity
Bona figured out that the legend of the Jade is nothing more than a hoax
The tribes forbid anyone from going to Ochkanatlan, believing it to be cursed after many adventurers died trying to find the Jade of Return
The Traveler eventually arrives to reunite Och-Kan’s tainted body and mind together, granting him his death, and releasing Ixlel from her imprisonment beneath Ochkanatlan
