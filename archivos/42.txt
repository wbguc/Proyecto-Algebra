NATLAN
Neuvillette calls it a land of dragons, and believes that he might not be welcomed there
Was once ruled by the Pyro Sovereign, Xiuhcoatl, who wielded the power of light, heat and wisdom
On the day of his birth, he gave life to his younger brother, Kukulkan, also known as Waxaklahun Ubah-Kan and later as Sage of the Stolen Flames
When the Heavenly Principles arrived in Teyvat and fought the Sovereigns, Xiuhcoatl fell into a false death and went into hiding
He eventually became decrepit and his powers declined
Natlan’s phlogiston-rich soil were regarded as Holy Soil (a/n: the same “Holy Soil” mentioned at Watatsumi Island) by the Heavenly Principles, who then sent Celestial Nails down to destroy and remake the land where they establish their rules
When Nibelung returned and fought against the Heavenly Principles, Xiuhcoatl and his kin awakened and joined in the fray, and it’s known as the War of Vengeance
The dragons took control of Natlan from the Angels, and human civilization collapsed
Any remaining Angels were devoured by the dragons
Dragon civilization thrived in Natlan, and they walled up their cities to keep the humans out. Phlogiston is controlled by the dragons using their language now called the Iridescent Inscriptions
They tried to strengthen the ley lines using Source Mechanisms, but were unable to navigate its intricate structure
Beneath the land of Natlan, a Seelie/Angel by the name Yohualtecuhtin established the Night Kingdom using the fragments of Natlan’s old Ley Lines to reconstruct a similar network with the help of Ronova, the Shade of Death
Xbalanque helped strengthen the realm
The Night Kingdom eventually became a land of souls
There is a celestial nail hanging here, speculated to be the same nail that destroyed the land when the Heavenly Principles deemed Natlan to be full of Holy Soil
The Sacred Flame serves as a conduit to the Night Kingdom, but also provide the future Pyro Archons significant knowledge and memory of the land
It’s also the foundation of Natlan’s resistance against the Abyss
Natlan’s Ley Lines are impossible to be connected to other nations due to the rules for the Ode of Resurrection
The dragons later took violence of the rule of law to maintain their kind’s might, and this caused Kukulkan to foresee his kin’s destruction and left them
He believed that their race can only be maintained if they share their knowledge of phlogiston with other races
He then took a primordial kindling from the ancient palace and left to travel, which earned him the title Sage of the Stolen Flames
He later found human settlements and were impressed by their tenacity, and shared the Primal Fire with the wisest and bravest human named Chaac
Kukulkan and Chaac created Hanan Pacha/Upper Sanctum using phlogiston to create a training ground for those who passed their trials
Those who passed the trials will be granted knowledge of phlogiston
They eventually formed the first human tribe, which eventually led to the Grand Alliance where all the tribes are united
Kukulkan believed that humanity will climb the ladder of civilization with the help of the Primal Fire, and unite the two races
Kukulkan and a human named Xbalanque eventually became friends, and made a pact, the two Golden Entreaties, with each other for the sake of humanity and dragonkind
They created the Hall of Parting Vows as testament of the trust between humans and dragons
Yupanqui also joined in their friendship
Around this time, Xbalanque saved Och-Kan from the dragons (read: Ochkanatlan for more)
Unknown whether Xbalanque is aware, but Kukulkan’s plan is for the Entreaties to be used in destroying the Abyss and rebuilding Natlan with a new human-dragon hybrid civilization per his belief of evolution belonging solely to the dragons
This is the reason why Och-Kan betrayed him, took his Golden Entreaty, and hides it within the Tonatiuh, knowing that his father can’t touch it due to his pact (read: Ochkanatlan for more)
Xiuhcoatl was eventually corrupted by the Abyss and became a corpse-like shadow of his former self
The war between humans and dragon eventually arrived, and Xbalanque eventually slain Xiuhcoatl at the Great Volcano of Tollan when the dragon began laying waste to Natlan and its Ley Lines after being corrupted by the Abyss
Xbalanque pierced the dragon’s eyes, which then split in two and left behind two white disc-shaped fragments
Both Xbalanque and Xiuhcoatl fell from a great height during the battle
Xiuhcoatl died, but Xbalanque was wreathed in flames and revived
Waxaklahun believed that his brother only died because he created the Huitzilopochtli array by modifying one of his eyes to serve as a control hub (read: Great Volcano of Tollan for more)
It’s said that specters stirred, evil came forth, and filth flood the land during the war
Some people believed that Xiuhcoatl still resides in the volcano, deep in hibernation, others believed it to be nothing more than an empty husk
Because of the in-fighting between dragons and humans, the Gods participating in the Archon War never reached Natlan, so the nation was never part of it
Xbalanque ascended as the Pyro Archon after slaying Xiuhcoatl
He bargained with Ronova to borrow her powers and establish the rules that will aid Natlan in fighting against the Abyss
The Ode of Resurrection and the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame was born thanks to him
He also set up the system and rules that will allow future humans to become Pyro Archons
For his use of her powers, death is the debt he must pay
Made a bet with her to return to Natlan for one day if the people managed to push back against the Abyss completely; he won the bet
He later become One Entombed with the Primal Fire
Around this time, a transparent flame called Turnfire came into existence. Those who studied it believed that it contains the remnants of Xbalanque’s Archon power
The victim will only be burned if they turn around
It’s currently burning within the Night Kingdom after the fall of Ochkanatlan and the death of Yupanqui
After it burned Yupanqui, it burnt through the ground and the ley lines, and into the Night Kingdom where it now remain
The humans who learned how to control phlogiston later used their abilities to turn against each other instead of improving their civilization, causing the Sage to seal Hanan Pacha and the Hall of Parting Vows
The Sage then withdrew from history, becoming a legend for the people of Natlan
According to Natlan’s legends, after the Sage banished the humans from Hanan Pacha, Chaac followed the guidance of the Lord of the Night and established permanent tribes within Natlan. At the end of his life, Chaac called out for his lost companions and lit the Primal Fire, the Lord of the Night answered him with the cries of the first Wayob
The non-corporeal Wayobs, consisting of will without form or thoughts, become the guardian of their respective tribes. They grant the humans protection and blessings, communicating via the tribe’s chief’s dreams
The Wayob are capable of seeing the past and the future
They grant Ancient Names to selected tribesmen
Due to the effect of the Abyss, the people of Natlan’s survival is tied to the Wayob, so leaving Natlan tends to be a long, complicated bureaucratic process which requires the Wayob’s approval
Otherwise they will suffer memory and emotional issues
It’s usually not granted
After the fall of Ochkanatlan and the Sage’s retreat from humanity, Natlan breaks off into the six major tribes known today:
Metzli, also known as People of the Springs
Huitzli, also known as Scions of the Canopy
Nanatzcayan, also known as Children of Echoes
Mictlan, also known as Masters of the Night-Wind
Tlalocan, also known as Flower-Feather Clan
Teteocan, also known as Collective of Plenty
After Xbalanque’s ascension and thanks to the framework he had established, many powerful yet regular humans manage to ascend as the Pyro Archon throughout the history of Natlan
The second Pyro Archon is the scarlet-eyed youth who overthrew Och-Kan, the mad King of Ochkanatlan
Another Archon named Cochanina was responsible in kicking out a dragon from the People of the Spring’s waters
Certain Saurians are born with a mark called the Flamelord’s Blessing, a mark related to Xiuhcoatl that grants them the power of ancient dragons. But the power is too much for modern Saurians to bear, which leads to their death or insanity (read: Great Volcano of Tollan for more)
The Wayobs and the Night Kingdom also reject this power
The only way to remove this mark is to bring the Sage’s and Xbalanque’s Golden Entreaties to the great volcano of Tollan and performing a baptism on the Saurian
No one has succeeded, and the tribes abandon this custom, simply caring for the affected Saurians until their eventual death
Monetoo are strange spirits born from ancient graffiti
Due to its weak ley line structure, Natlan was hit the worst during the Cataclysm, which ended up corrupting the Night Kingdom to the point where the light of the Wayobs’ totems dimmed
The heroes calculated that they have 500 years before the Night Kingdom falls completely to the Abyss (read: Cataclysm for more info)
The corrosion from the Cataclysm resulted in a massive Pyro energy shortage
Il Capitano aided Natlan against the Abyss after being granted kindness by Flower-Feather Clan’s Chief Ayizu after escaping Khaenri’ah (read: The Harbingers for more)
After repelling the Abyss, Mavuika devised a plan to return to Natlan 500 years later when the tribes have regained their strength in order to defeat the Abyss for good
She also wanted the successors to the heroes’ Ancient Name to fight alongside her in 500 years
Present day during the Pilgrimage, and after Mavuika’s return, the Ode of Resurrection failed to bring Kachina back after she fell in the Night Warden War. This is due to the rampant corruption within the Night Kingdom
She was later successfully revived, but Mavuika suspended the Pilgrimage until further notice
Because of the corruption as well, only three of the six heroes’ memories from 500 years ago reawakened so far. Mualani’s Ancient Name was awakened during Kachina’s rescue
The other three are Xilonen, Iansan, and Kinich
Il Capitano, who became dissatisfied with Mavuika’s gamble on the heroes’ reawakening and the deterioration of the Night Kingdom, tried to fight her for the Pyro Gnosis to protect Natlan, but he unfortunately lost the battle
He was saved by Ororon, and they went on to search for the Source Mechanism that was supposedly belonged to the dragons
Had he proceed with his plan, all of Natlan’s history will disappear and leave them vulnerable for future Abyssal attacks
Yohualcutin, who had been slumbering since the Night Kingdom’s establishment, woke up when Capitano activated the Source Mechanism
She’s the only one who can use it after creating the Night Kingdom under Ronova’s guidance
Her awakening caused Ororon to almost be possessed by Capitano’s former soldier from Khaenri’ah, but he fought against it and awakened his Ancient Name
Natlan is once more attacked by the Abyss in a massive nation-wide invasion, as 500 years have passed and the heroes have yet to reawaken. The Flower-Feather Clan is hit the worst because of its lack of hero until Chasca’s Ancient Name activated upon Chuychu’s death
The six heroes channel Ronova’s power unto Mavuika who stops the Abyss and forced it to retreat into the Night Kingdom (read: Mavuika for more info)
The battle revealed pieces of broken moon above the Stadium of the Sacred Flame which can only be seen in Natlan
The fragments are known as Ancient Moon Remains, relics of the ancient dragons’ Secret Source technology
A small squad of Dottore’s subordinates infiltrated Natlan’s border around the same time it appeared
After the Traveler received their Ancient Name, they and Mavuika went down into the Night Kingdom to completely purge the Abyss aka Gosoythoth which took the form of the Pyro Sovereign
The two overpowered Gosoythoth and finally won when Yohualtecuhtin granted Traveler the power of Pyro
Natlan’s Ley Lines and Night Kingdom are restored after the battle against Gosoythoth, allowing the people to leave the border without worry
Yohualtecuhtin couldn’t restore it on her own as her lifespan was near depleted, hence why Mavuika wanted to give her life to sustain the Sacred Flame and Yohualtecuhtin
This is also due to her death debt with Ronova after using her powers
Mavuika’s life force will sustain the Flame for a few hundred years while future Pyro Archons seek new sources of fire
Capitano instead took Mavuika’s position and merged his life force with Yohualtecuhtin instead, allowing her to essentially live forever (read: Harbingers for more)
Natlan now enters an era of peace
The Pilgrimage is now used to showcase the thrill of competition rather than to select warriors
It’s still a popular tournament
