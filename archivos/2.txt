TRAVELER’S TWIN (return to top)

First to awake upon arriving in Teyvat with no memories whatsoever, and mysteriously left the spaceship without opening its doors
Listed in Irminsul as a Khaenri’ahn and not a Descender
The end of their journey’s recording was blurred by an unknown entity
The twin only arrived because the Heavens ‘responded to the summoning’. No further details given
Was imprisoned within a coffin at the Temple of Space
Lived in Khaenri’ah as their Prince/Princess for years along with The Jester who was the Royal Mage, and Dainsleif as the Twilight Sword
Their memories slowly return as time passes, including that of the Traveler
They searched the whole planet but found no trace of the Traveler
Was called a Savior when they were first granted the title of Prince/Princess by the Vinster King and the entire Court of Khaenri’ah
They said with the twin there, all pain and suffering would be washed away, and the future of Khaenri’ah would be brighter
Dainsleif calls it a cruel irony as it merely stood for the Vinster King’s lust for power
Was used as a vessel by which unlimited amount of Abyssal power could be absorbed, which then caused the Cataclysm
Was ready to do their part as the Prince/Princess and defend Khaenri’ah during the Cataclysm, but then Direidyth brought the Hibernation Chamber Key to the twin, and the twin abandoned Khaenri’ah as a result
The twin decided to see the Traveler, only to find them still sleeping with the Key on their person
The twin grabbed their sibling and ran, which led to the beginning of the game
Afterwards, they traveled with Dainsleif in search for their fate, but parted halfway for reasons unknown and now views Dainsleif as an enemy
Arrived at a place called ‘The Sea of Flowers at the End’ after they completed their journey
This is the reason why the twin can’t continue their journey between worlds with the Traveler, because the Traveler once mentioned wanting to find a place in the universe where the one flower is in full bloom
Later, they lead the Abyss Order, founded by Chlothar Alberich, to fight against destiny and to revive Khaenri’ah under a plan called Loom of Fate
It’s now completed after retrieving the eye of the first Field-Tiller from Dainsleif
They made many plans in order to fight against destiny
Tried to weaponize Dvalin by poisoning his mind but failed when the Traveler purified him
Tried to mechanized Osial’s body using the Defiled Statue and the eye of the first Field-Tiller only for Dainsleif to snatch the eye away the first time
Tried to cleanse the curse plaguing the hilichurls using the upside down fountain in the Chasm but failed when Halfdan destroyed the machine
During the events of Caribert, the twin doesn’t know how to utilize the Loom of Fate to its full potential, but believes there is still time before the Heavenly Principal awakens
Caribert died at the age of 8 during its construction, but a remnant of his consciousness remained to mature
This allows him to implant false memories for the people of Vimara Village and within Dainsleif, making him believe that he had given the eye of the first Field Tiller to the Traveler
The Loom of Fate is able to weave Ley Lines of its own at its full potential, and will lose its abilities to implant memories when that time comes
Currently, the twin managed to grow a realm based on the Khaenri’ah of 500 years ago, superimposing it over Natlan’s present-day spacetime in an Atlas of the New World, a product of the Loom of Fate
The Inteyvat was used as a base, and the twin received the Lord of the Night’s permission once she’s satisfied that it won’t have any real effect on Natlan, though she imposed an additional condition which she kept to herself
The new Khaenri’ah is enriched with memories of the old drawn from the Abyss Order and Dainsleif who was tricked into reminiscing the old Khaenri’ah
This is how the Direidyth of 500 years ago managed to meet with the Traveler, who then gave her the Hibernation Chamber Key which set off the chain reaction
The twin sees it as a mocking from Istaroth, as their actions 500 years in the future is the one that set everything in motion
The twin will one day find a suitable host location for it so Khaenri’ah can rise again
