MARE JIVARI (return to top)

Located at the westernmost part of Natlan in a very remote location far away from any tribe, where the wind does not blow, and contains a sea of scorching lava
It was born after the battle between the six heroes of Natlan and the Abyss 500 years ago
The ashes of the monsters and warriors who fought there covered the entire battlefield
The corruption of Mare Jivari is tied to time itself; a calamity present in every moment that will ever be
To prevent its corruption from spreading out of Tenochtzitoc, Barbatos blew it off the timeline at the cost of several souls unable to return to Mondstadt
Has a phoenix dwelling within it that was worshiped by humans
The Bakunawa that resided in Mare Jivari lost some flesh in some incident, and was kept by the Hexenzirkel as an alchemic reagent
It was later given to Albedo to give Durin a human form
A Sage known as Lavawalker traversed the area, going in and out of it without trouble for a hundred years until the sea of flames finally burned him to ash
The Wanderer’s Troupe set foot there a thousand years ago while ‘performing’ for their foes
They also fought Bakunawa there, and carved the Runestone from its body
Stanley saved a greenhorn adventurer named Hans Archibald from an evil beast and perished there. Hans Archibald took his name and recount Stanley’s tales as his own to keep the man’s memory alive
Barbatos eventually took Stanley’s spirit from Hans in his story quest
Stanley was devoured by the monsters within Mare Jivari
According to his notes, the scents of the monsters are similar to the ones in Dragonspine
‘Nocht’ and ‘Nanas’ were two people of Tenochtzitoc who helped him before his death
Bennett’s parents and their adventuring team wandered into Mare Jivari and died at the hands of the monsters within it
Bennett was the only survivor and was saved by an adventurer who then took him to Mondstadt
Because of his separation from Natlan’s leylines, Bennett became the unlucky boy we know today, until his visit to Natlan where the past was revealed
Nahida confirms that the area is empty despite never seeing it as it’s one of the many areas beyond her reach
Enjou is looking for history regarding Mare Jivari, which he said can never be accessed unless Bakunawa’s power is obtained
Kinich says that it’s an area that’s hard to get access to
Many adventurers have tried to conquer the area, but most of them perished
20-30 years ago, people started talking about an enormous, mountain-sized creature roaming Mare Jivari. Most likely the Bakunawa
The entire Mare Jivari vanished without a trace shortly afterwards. Venti says someone else is more suited to tell this story
